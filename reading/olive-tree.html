The Olive Tree

Reading Lesson

📖 Reading Passage

The olive tree is one of the oldest trees in the world. It is very important in Palestine and many Mediterranean countries. People use olives to make olive oil, soap, and delicious food.


Olive trees can live for hundreds of years. Farmers take care of the trees during the whole year. In autumn, families gather together to pick olives and celebrate the harvest season.

📝 Vocabulary

Olive

A small fruit used for oil and food.

Harvest

The season of collecting crops.

Farmer

A person who grows plants and trees.

Mediterranean

Countries near the Mediterranean Sea.

❓ Comprehension Questions

  1. Why is the olive tree important?
  2. What do people make from olives?
  3. When do families pick olives?
  4. How long can olive trees live?
