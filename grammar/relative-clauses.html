⭐ The Pattern

A relative clause comes right after the noun it describes.
Choose the pronoun based on what that noun is: person / thing / place / possession.

🧠 The 5 Relative Pronouns — Full Rules
الجدول الكامل للضمائر الموصولة مع الأمثلة.
PronounUsed forExample
who people 👤 The teacher who taught me was very kind.
which things / animals 📦 The book which I bought was expensive.
that people or things
(defining clauses only)		 The film that I watched was great.
The boy that won is my brother.
where places 📍 The city where I was born is Ramallah.
whose possession 🔑
(like his/her/their)		 The girl whose bag is red is my sister.
🧠 Defining vs Non-Defining Clauses

✅ Defining Clause

Essential info — removes it and the sentence loses meaning.
No commas.
that is OK.

"The student who won the prize is from Grade 9."
(We need to know which student.)

ℹ️ Non-Defining Clause

Extra info — remove it and the sentence still makes sense.
Always use commas.
No that.

"Ahmad, who is my best friend, lives in Nablus."
(We already know which Ahmad.)
الجملة التعريفية ضرورية ولا تحتاج فواصل. الجملة غير التعريفية معلومة إضافية وتحتاج فواصل ولا تستخدم that.
🧠 How to Combine Two Sentences
3 خطوات لدمج جملتين باستخدام ضمير موصول.
  1. Find the shared noun (repeated in both sentences).
  2. Choose the right relative pronoun (who / which / that / where / whose).
  3. Replace the repeated noun in the second sentence with the pronoun and join.

📌 I have a friend. + The friend lives in Gaza.

Step 1: shared noun = "a friend / the friend"

Step 2: friend = person → who

✅ I have a friend who lives in Gaza.
