Past Continuous

🎯 Grade 9 ⏱️ ~30 min الماضي المستمر

🚀 Freeze the Moment!

🎥 Imagine pressing pause on a video of yesterday. At 8 o'clock last night, what were you doing? Maybe you were eating, your sister was studying, and the TV was playing.
We use the Past Continuous to describe an action in progress at a moment in the past.

📌 الطبخ كان جارياً عندما وصلتُ — اختَر الصيغة الصحيحة:

"When I arrived, my mother _____ (cook) dinner."

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form the Past Continuous: was / were + verb-ing
  • Describe an action in progress at a past moment
  • Use when + Past Simple to show an interruption
  • Use while / as for two actions at the same time
  • Make negatives and questions in the Past Continuous

🔍 The Interruption Pattern

📌 جملة فيها حدثان — أيّهما كان طويلاً جارياً وأيّهما قصير قاطعه؟
Long action (in progress)Short action (interruption)
I was watching TVwhen the phone rang.
She was cookingwhen I arrived.
They were sleepingwhen the alarm went off.
📌 أيّ زمن نستخدم للحدث القصير الذي يقاطع الحدث الطويل؟

🧠 The Rule

Form: Subject + was / were + verb-ing
الصيغة: الفاعل + was/were + الفعل + ing
Subjectwas / wereExample
I / He / She / ItwasShe was reading.
You / We / TheywereThey were playing.

🧠 When Do We Use It?

UseExample
Action in progress at a specific past timeAt 8pm, I was studying.
Interrupted by a shorter action (when + Past Simple)I was cooking when he arrived.
Two parallel actions (while / as)She was reading while he was writing.
Setting the scene in a storyThe sun was shining and birds were singing.

🧠 Negative & Question

TypeStructureExample
Negative ❌Subject + wasn't / weren't + verb-ingHe wasn't listening.
Question ❓Was / Were + subject + verb-ing?Were you sleeping?
Short answer ✅Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.Yes, she was.
💡 when + Past Simple (short action)  ·  while / as + Past Continuous (long action)
when تأتي مع الماضي البسيط (الحدث القصير) · while/as تأتي مع الماضي المستمر (الحدث الطويل)

✏️ Level 1 — Choose the Correct Form

📌 اختَر الصيغة الصحيحة — هل الحدث كان جارياً أم قصيراً قاطعاً؟

1. When she called, I _____ (read) a book.

2. While he _____ (cook), she _____ (set) the table.

3. They _____ (play) outside when it started to rain.

✏️ Level 3 — Make Questions & Negatives

📌 حوّل كل جملة كما هو مطلوب، ثم اضغط "Check":

1. Make a question: She was sleeping.

2. Make negative: They were watching TV.

3. Combine (use while): He was studying. + The phone rang.

🗣️ Freeze Frame — 8pm Last Night

📌 اكتب ٤ جمل تصف ما كان يفعله أفراد عائلتك الساعة ٨ مساء أمس:

✏️ At 8pm last night, what was everyone in your family doing?

e.g. My father was watching the news. My sister was doing her homework…

🗣️ Interruptions

📌 اكتب ٤ جمل: حدث طويل كان جارياً + حدث قصير قاطعه (when):

✏️ Write 4 sentences: something in progress + something that interrupted it.

e.g. I was sleeping when the alarm rang. We were eating when the guest arrived…

🗣️ Challenge — Set the Scene

📌 تحدٍّ: اكتب بداية قصة (٥ جمل) تصف المشهد، مستخدماً الماضي المستمر:

🏆 Begin a story by describing the scene. Use at least 4 Past Continuous sentences.
