🧩 Pattern Summary

Most numbers: add -th (four→fourth, six→sixth...)
Special: five→fifth, eight→eighth, nine→ninth, twelve→twelfth
Irregular: one→first, two→second, three→third

📅 Using Ordinals with Dates

We say: the + ordinal + of + month

My birthday is on the fifth of March.
Today is the second of November.

نقول: the fifth of March — الخامس من مارس

🏆 Position & Order

We also use ordinals for position in a race, list, grade, or family:

  • Rami came first in the race. 🥇
  • I am in the fifth grade.
  • She is the third child in her family.

🗣️ Speaking Rounds
تحدّث مع زميلك! استخدم الأعداد الترتيبية في جملك.
Round 1 · Your position

Tell your partner: "In my family, I am the ___ child."

Round 2 · Months

Ask your partner: "What is the ___ month of the year?" (use a number between 1–12)

Round 3 · Your birthday

Say: "My birthday is on the ___ of ___." (Use ordinal + month name)

Round 4 · Class race

Imagine a class race. Say who came first, second, and third using your classmates' names.
