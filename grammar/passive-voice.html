Passive Voice 🏭

المبني للمجهول

🎯 Grade 9 ⏱️ ~40 min 🏭 Processes & Focus
🚀 Who Did It? Does It Matter?
Sometimes we care about WHAT happened — not WHO did it.
📰 Headline: "The old bridge was destroyed."
We focus on the bridge — not on who destroyed it.
🫒 Fact: "Olive oil is produced in Palestine."
The focus is on olive oil — not on the farmers.
🏫 Notice: "English is spoken in more than 50 countries."
The speaker is not important — English is.
ما الفرق بين "كتبتُ الرسالة" (مبني للمعلوم) و"كُتبت الرسالة" (مبني للمجهول)؟ اليوم نتعلم كيف نحوّل التركيز في الإنجليزية!

⭐ The Passive Formula

Subject + be (correct tense) + past participle (+ by + agent)

🧠 Passive Voice — All Tenses
جدول المبني للمجهول في كل الأزمنة.
TenseActivePassive
Present Simple They make soap here. Soap is made here.
Past Simple She wrote the poem. The poem was written by her.
Present Perfect They have planted 1000 trees. 1000 trees have been planted.
Future (will) They will build a new school. A new school will be built.
Present Continuous They are repairing the road. The road is being repaired.
🧠 How to Transform: 3 Steps
3 خطوات لتحويل الجملة من المبني للمعلوم إلى المبني للمجهول.
  1. Move the object to the subject position.
  2. Change the verb to be + past participle (keep the same tense).
  3. Move the original subject after by — or drop it if unknown/unimportant.

📌 Workers build houses every year.

Step 1: Object "houses" → new subject

Step 2: build (Present Simple) → are built

✅ Houses are built (by workers) every year.

🧠 When to Use the Passive
🤷

Agent unknown

My bag was stolen.
(We don't know who.)

🔬

Scientific / Formal

The samples were tested in the lab.

📰

News / Focus

A new hospital will be opened next month.

✏️ Level 3 — Make It Passive
حوّل كل جملة إلى المبني للمجهول. اكتب فقط الفعل المبني للمجهول في الفراغ.
1) People speak Arabic here. → Arabic here. (Pres. Simple)
2) They built this mosque in 1400. → This mosque in 1400. (Past Simple)
3) They will deliver the books tomorrow. → The books tomorrow. (Future)
4) Scientists have found a new cure. → A new cure by scientists. (Pres. Perfect)
🎉 Level 3 done! Excellent transformation skills!
