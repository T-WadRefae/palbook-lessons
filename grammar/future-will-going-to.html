Future: will & going to

🎯 Grades 5, 7 & 9 ⏱️ ~35 min المستقبل: will و going to

🚀 What Does the Future Hold?

Imagine you're holding a crystal ball 🔮 — you can see what's coming!
In English, we have two main ways to talk about the future. Sometimes you decide right now; sometimes you planned it long ago. Can you feel the difference?

👦 Khalid:
"I 'm going to study engineering — I applied last month."
👧 Dina:
"Oh, I just dropped my pen. I 'll pick it up."

Can you spot the difference? One was planned before, one is decided right now.

📌 اختَر الإجابة الصحيحة — أيّ صيغة مستقبل تناسب كلّ جملة؟

1. "Look at those dark clouds! It _____ rain any minute."


2. "I didn't bring a pen. Don't worry — I _____ lend you mine."

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Use will for predictions, promises, and spontaneous decisions
  • Use going to for prior plans, intentions, and evidence-based predictions
  • Form negatives: won't / am not / isn't / aren't going to
  • Form questions: Will…? / Are you going to…?
  • Choose the right form based on context clues

🔍 What Do You Notice?

Read these conversations carefully. Who planned ahead? Who decided just now?

Conversation A — A Surprise Offer

👧 Sara:
"This bag is so heavy! I can't carry it."
👦 Omar:
"Don't worry! I will carry it for you."

→ Omar just heard the problem and decided right now.

Conversation B — An Arranged Plan

👧 Rima:
"What are your plans for this summer?"
👦 Jamal:
"I 'm going to visit my grandparents. I already bought the ticket."

→ Jamal decided this before the conversation — he even bought a ticket!

Conversation C — A Prediction

🌦️
"Look at those clouds — it 's going to rain!"
"I think robots will do most jobs in 2050."

→ Evidence visible NOW → going to. General prediction, no evidence → will.

🧠 The Rules — will vs going to

🔵 will + base verb

  • ⚡ Spontaneous decision
    "I'll get the door!"
  • 🤝 Promise
    "I will never lie to you."
  • 🔮 General prediction
    "Robots will replace many jobs."
  • 💁 Offer / request
    "Will you help me?"

🟡 am/is/are going to

  • 📋 Prior plan / intention
    "I'm going to study tonight."
  • 🌦️ Prediction with evidence
    "It's going to rain — see the clouds?"
  • ✅ Decision made before
    "She's going to move to Ramallah."

🧠 Positive, Negative & Question Forms

+ PositiveI/You/He/She/We/They will goI am going to go

– NegativeI won't (will not) goI am not going to go

? QuestionWill you go?Are you going to go?

Short answerYes, I will. / No, I won't.Yes, I am. / No, I 'm not.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid:
  • ✗ I will to go. → ✓ I will go. (no "to" after will!)
  • ✗ She is going to goes. → ✓ She is going to go. (base verb!)
  • ✗ They going to travel. → ✓ They are going to travel.
📌 تذكّر: لا "to" بعد will، واستخدم المصدر دائماً (base verb).

🗣️ Speaking — Talk About Your Future

📌 تحدَّث مع زميلك عن المواضيع التالية — استخدم will و going to في حديثك:

🎯 Round 1 — This Weekend

Tell your partner 2 things you are going to do this weekend (prior plans).

e.g. "I'm going to visit my cousins on Friday."

🔮 Round 2 — Predictions for 2050

Give 3 predictions about what the world will look like in 2050.

e.g. "Cars will fly." / "People will live on Mars."

💬 Round 3 — Classroom Situations

Your teacher offers you a piece of chocolate — respond spontaneously using will.

e.g. "Thank you! I'll save it for later!"
