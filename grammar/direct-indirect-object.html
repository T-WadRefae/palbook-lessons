What is an Object?

An object is a noun or pronoun that receives the action of a verb.

المفعول به هو الاسم أو الضمير الذي يقع عليه فعل الفاعل

She reads a book

↑ "a book" receives the action of reading

🎯

Direct Object

The direct object answers: "What?" or "Whom?"

المفعول به المباشر يجيب على سؤال: ماذا؟ أو مَن؟

I bought a car 🚗

What did I buy? → a car

She called her friend 👩

Whom did she call? → her friend

🎁

Indirect Object

The indirect object answers: "To whom?" or "For whom?"

المفعول به غير المباشر يجيب على سؤال: لِمَن؟ أو من أجل مَن؟

I gave my mom flowers 💐

To whom? → my mom | What? → flowers

He sent me a message 📩

To whom? → me | What? → a message

⚖️

The Key Difference

🎯 Direct Object

  • ✓ Receives the action directly
  • ✓ Answers: What? Whom?
  • ✓ Comes after the verb

يستقبل الفعل مباشرة

🎁 Indirect Object

  • ✓ Receives the direct object
  • ✓ Answers: To whom? For whom?
  • ✓ Comes between verb & D.O.

يستقبل المفعول به المباشر

Word Order:

Subject + Verb + I.O. + D.O.

🔄

Using TO & FOR

The indirect object can come after the direct object using TO or FOR

يمكن وضع المفعول غير المباشر بعد المباشر باستخدام to أو for

Standard order:

I gave her a gift

↓ Same meaning ↓

I gave a gift to her

📤

When to use TO

Use TO with verbs that show transfer or direction

استخدم TO مع أفعال النقل والتوجيه

✉️

give, send, show, tell, teach, offer, lend, pass, write

She sent a letter to her grandma

He taught grammar to the students

🎀

When to use FOR

Use FOR with verbs that show benefit or creation

استخدم FOR مع أفعال الفائدة والصنع

🛠️

buy, make, get, find, cook, build, save, order

Mom made a cake for us 🎂

I bought flowers for my wife 💐

⚡

TO vs FOR: Quick Guide

TO 📤

نقل شيء لشخص

Something moves TO someone

give to, send to, show to

FOR 🎀

عمل شيء لأجل شخص

Something done FOR someone's benefit

buy for, make for, cook for

💡 Tip: If you can rephrase with "in order to help," use FOR

إذا أمكن إعادة الصياغة بـ "لمساعدة" استخدم FOR

✍️

Practice Examples

The teacher showed the class a video

= showed a video to the class

Dad built us a treehouse 🏡

= built a treehouse for us

She read the children a story 📖

= read a story to the children
