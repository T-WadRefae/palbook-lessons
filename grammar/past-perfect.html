Past Perfect

🎯 Grade 9 ⏱️ ~30 min الماضي التام

🚀 Rewind the Story!

⏮️ You arrive at the cinema… but the film has already begun — it started before you got there. To show that one past action happened before another past action, we use the Past Perfect.

📌 المغادرة حدثت قبل وصولي — اختَر الصيغة الصحيحة:

"When I arrived, she _____ (already / leave)."

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form the Past Perfect: had + past participle
  • Show that one past action happened before another
  • Use by the time / before / after / already / never … before
  • Make negatives and questions in the Past Perfect
  • Order events correctly when telling a story

🔍 The Order of Events

📌 الماضي التام دائماً للحدث الأقدم، والماضي البسيط للحدث الأحدث — لاحظ الخط الزمني:

"By the time we arrived, the film had started."

Film had started We arrived
📌 الحدث الأقدم (film) يكون بصيغة...؟

🧠 The Rule

Form: Subject + had + past participle (3rd form) — same for ALL subjects
الصيغة: الفاعل + had + التصريف الثالث — لجميع الفاعلين
Subjecthad + participleExample
I / You / He / She / We / Theyhad eatenThey had eaten before noon.
Contraction'd + participleShe'd left. (= She had left)

🧠 When Do We Use It?

UseExample
An action completed BEFORE another past actionWhen she arrived, I had already eaten.
With by the timeBy the time he woke up, we had left.
A reason in the pastHe was tired because he hadn't slept.
With never … beforeI had never seen snow before that day.

🧠 Negative & Question

TypeStructureExample
Negative ❌Subject + hadn't + participleShe hadn't finished.
Question ❓Had + subject + participle?Had you ever seen it before?
Short answer ✅Yes, I had. / No, they hadn't.Yes, she had.
💡 The OLDER action → Past Perfect (had + participle). The NEWER action → Past Simple.
الحدث الأقدم → الماضي التام (had + التصريف الثالث) · الحدث الأحدث → الماضي البسيط

2. He was tired because he _____ (work) all night.

✏️ Level 3 — Combine the Events

📌 ادمج الحدثين في جملة واحدة مستخدماً الماضي التام للحدث الأقدم:

1. She left. → Then I arrived. (use: by the time)

2. Make a question: They had arrived.

3. They didn't eat. → Then the meeting ended. (use: hadn't … before)

🗣️ Before That Moment

📌 اكتب ٤ جمل عن أشياء كانت قد حدثت قبل لحظة معينة في الماضي:

✏️ Write 4 sentences about things that had already happened before a past moment.

e.g. By the time I woke up, Mum had already made breakfast. When I got to school, the lesson had started…

🗣️ First Times

📌 اكتب ٣ جمل عن تجارب لم تكن قد جرّبتها من قبل، مستخدماً never … before:

✏️ Write 3 sentences using "had never … before".

e.g. Before last summer, I had never swum in the sea.

🏆 Tell a short story about a past day. Use at least 3 Past Perfect sentences to show what had happened earlier.
