🧠 The Rule

Form: Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing
الصيغة: الفاعل + am/is/are + الفعل + ing
SubjectTo BeExample
IamI am studying.
He / She / ItisShe is dancing.
You / We / TheyareThey are laughing.

🧠 Negative & Question

TypeStructureExample
Negative ❌Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ingHe isn't sleeping.
Question ❓Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?Are you listening?
Short answer ✅Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.Yes, they are.

🧠 Spelling Rules for -ing

📌 انتبه لقواعد الإملاء عند إضافة -ing إلى الأفعال:
Most verbs → just add -ing
play → playing
read → reading
معظم الأفعال: أضف -ing مباشرة
Ends in -e → drop e, add -ing
write → writing
dance → dancing
ينتهي بـ e: احذف e وأضف -ing
Short CVC → double last letter
run → running
swim → swimming
حرف+حركة+حرف قصير: ضاعف الأخير
Ends in -ie → change ie to y, add -ing
lie → lying  |  die → dying  |  tie → tying
ينتهي بـ ie: حوّل ie إلى y ثم أضف -ing

🧠 Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Present SimplePresent Continuous
Habits / routines
عادات ومواقف دائمة		Actions happening NOW
أحداث تجري الآن
I play football every day.I am playing football now.
She reads before bed.She is reading right now.
They go to school.They are going to school now.
