🎯 Grades 7 & 9 ⏱️ ~35 min المضارع التام

🚀 The Bridge to Now

🍰 Have you ever tasted knaafeh?   📝 Have you finished your homework yet?
These questions link the past to now — the result still matters at this moment. That is exactly when we use the Present Perfect.

📌 انظر إلى الموقف واختَر الجملة الصحيحة. (الحدث وقع في الماضي لكن نتيجته مهمة الآن):

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form the Present Perfect: have / has + past participle
  • Use just, already, yet correctly
  • Talk about life experiences with ever / never
  • Use for and since for duration
  • Tell the difference between Present Perfect and Past Simple

🔍 Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

📌 قارِن بين الجملتين — أيّهما يذكر وقتاً محدّداً في الماضي؟ وأيّهما يربط الماضي بالحاضر؟
Past Simple (وقت محدّد)Present Perfect (صلة بالحاضر)
I visited Jerusalem in 2019.I have visited Jerusalem. (when? not important)
She ate breakfast at 7am.She has just eaten breakfast.
They finished yesterday.They have already finished.
📌 أيّ زمن نستخدمه عندما لا نذكر وقتاً محدّداً والنتيجة مهمة الآن؟

🧠 The Rule

Form: Subject + have / has + past participle (3rd form)
الصيغة: الفاعل + have/has + التصريف الثالث للفعل
SubjectAuxiliaryExample
I / You / We / TheyhaveI have finished my work.
He / She / IthasShe has eaten already.

🧠 Where Do the Signal Words Go?

📌 لكل كلمة دلالة موضعها الخاص في الجملة — انتبه جيداً:
MarkerPositionExample
justafter have/hasI have just arrived.
alreadyafter have/hasShe has already left.
yetat the end (neg. / question)I haven't finished yet. · Have you eaten yet?
ever / neverafter have/hasHave you ever been to Gaza? · I have never seen snow.

🧠 Negative & Question

TypeStructureExample
Negative ❌Subject + haven't / hasn't + participleHe hasn't arrived yet.
Question ❓Have / Has + subject + participle?Have you finished?
Short answer ✅Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.Yes, they have.

🧠 for vs. since

📌 نستخدم "for" مع مدة زمنية، و "since" مع نقطة بداية محدّدة:
for + مدةsince + نقطة بداية
for five yearssince 2020
for two hourssince Monday
I have lived here for five years.She has studied English since 2020.
