⚡ Irregular Verbs — Something Different

Now look at these past forms. Do they follow the -ed rule?

Base formPast Simple
I go to school.I went to school.
She eats breakfast.She ate breakfast.
We see the film.We saw the film.
He buys bread.He bought bread.
💡 Did you spot it?

Irregular verbs do NOT add -ed. They change completely to a new word.
There is no rule — you must memorise them!

الأفعال الشاذة لا تتبع قاعدة -ed — لكل فعل شاذ شكله الخاص في الماضي.
مثال: go → went، eat → ate، see → saw. يجب حفظها.

❓ Negatives & Questions — Spot the Pattern

How do we make negatives and questions in Past Simple?

Affirmative ✅Negative ❌Question ❓
I went. I didn't go. Did you go?
She ate. She didn't eat. Did she eat?
💡 Key discovery!

With didn't and Did, the verb goes back to its base form.
❌ I didn't went   ✅ I didn't go

مع النفي والسؤال في الماضي البسيط نستخدم did / didn't، والفعل الرئيسي يرجع لصيغته الأصلية.
❌ She didn't went   ✅ She didn't go

📌 When Do We Use It?

Use Past Simple for:
✅ Completed actions — things that started and finished in the past.
✅ Stories & narration — "First… then… finally…"
✅ Past habits — things you used to do regularly.
Ahmad visited his grandmother last Friday. (completed) He woke up, ate breakfast, then left. (story)
نستخدم الماضي البسيط: للأحداث المنتهية — القصص والحكايات — والعادات الماضية.

📝 Linking Words for Stories

Use these words to make your story flow naturally:
First → Then / Next → After that → Finally
First, I woke up. Then I ate breakfast. After that, I went to school. Finally, I played football.
