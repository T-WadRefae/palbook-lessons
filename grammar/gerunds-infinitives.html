المصدر بـ ing مقابل to + الفعل

🎯 Grades 8 & 9 ⏱️ ~40 min 🪧 -ing vs to
🪧 Two Lanes — Which Way?
Some verbs are followed by -ing (a gerund); others by to + verb (an infinitive).
🔵 -ing lane
I enjoy playing football.
🟢 to lane
I want to play football.
بعض الأفعال يتبعها الفعل بصيغة ing (gerund)، وأخرى يتبعها to + الفعل (infinitive). المفتاح: الفعل الأول هو الذي يقرّر المسار. لنكتشف!

📌 The Two Lanes — Common Verbs
قائمة سريعة بأشهر الأفعال في كل مسار.
🔵 verb + -ing
enjoy · finish · avoid · mind · keep · practise · suggest · miss
+ after prepositions: good at swimming
🟢 verb + to
want · need · decide · hope · plan · would like · promise · agree · learn · offer
💛 Both (little change): like · love · hate · start · begin — "I like swimming" = "I like to swim."
🔍 What Decides the Lane?
Look at the FIRST verb. It chooses -ing or to.
انظر إلى الفعل الأول — هو الذي يحدّد المسار (ing أو to).
🔵 -ing
She finished doing her homework.
They avoid eating junk food.
🟢 to
He hopes to win the match.
We agreed to help.
💡 Two more -ing rules:
1) After a preposition → -ing: interested in learning, good at drawing.
2) As the subject → -ing: Swimming is good exercise.

⭐ The Pattern

🔵 enjoy/finish/avoid + -ing · after a preposition → -ing
🟢 want/decide/hope + to + verb

🧠 The Full Rules
القواعد الكاملة للمصدر بـ ing و to + الفعل.
🔵 Gerund (verb + -ing)
• After: enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, keep, practise, suggest, miss
• After a preposition: good at, interested in, before, after
• As the subject: Reading is fun.
🟢 Infinitive (to + verb)
• After: want, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, agree, learn, offer
• After would like/love: I would like to go.
• To show purpose (why): I went out to buy bread.
🧠 Important Notes
⚠️ The FIRST verb decides: ✗ I want going · ✓ I want to go. ✗ I enjoy to swim · ✓ I enjoy swimming. الفعل الأول هو الذي يحدّد المسار.
✅ like / love / hate / start / begin can take BOTH with almost the same meaning:
"I like cooking." = "I like to cook." هذه الأفعال تقبل الشكلين بمعنى واحد تقريبًا.
💡 Spelling of the -ing: make → making (drop e) · run → running (double) · study → studying. إملاء ing: نحذف e، ونضاعف الحرف الساكن أحيانًا.
