🌅 Your Daily Routine

A warm-up to get you thinking — 2 to 3 minutes.

Every day you do the same things again and again.
You wake up → you brush your teeth → you go to school.

فكّر: ما الأشياء التي تفعلها كل يوم قبل ذهابك إلى المدرسة؟ شاركها مع زميلك.

💭 Think About It

What do you do every morning?

I wake up. I eat breakfast. I go to school.
These are habits — things we do again and again!

❓ Negatives & Questions — Spot the Pattern

Now look at what happens with negatives and questions.

Affirmative ✅Negative ❌Question ❓
I play. I don't play. Do you play?
She plays. She doesn't play. Does she play?
💡 Did you spot it?

With negatives and questions, the verb goes back to its base form — no -s!
She doesn't plays  ·  Does she plays?

الخلاصة: مع النفي والسؤال نستخدم do / does كفعل مساعد، والفعل الرئيسي يبقى بصيغته الأصلية بدون -s.
❌ She doesn't plays   ✅ She doesn't play

📌 When Do We Use It?

Use Present Simple for:
✅ Habits & routines — things you do regularly.
✅ General facts & truths — things that are always true.
✅ Fixed timetables — school starts at 8.
I go to school every day. (habit) The sun rises in the east. (fact) The train leaves at 7 a.m. (timetable)
نستخدم المضارع البسيط: للعادات اليومية — الحقائق الثابتة — الجداول الزمنية.

📐 How to Form It

SubjectVerb formExample
I / You / We / Theybase verbI play. We study.
He / She / Itverb + -sShe plays. He eats.
He/She/It + go/do/watch…verb + -esHe goes. She watches.
He/She/It + study/try…y → -iesShe studies. He tries.
القاعدة الذهبية: مع he / she / it نضيف -s أو -es. إذا انتهى الفعل بـ y قبلها ساكن: y → -ies.

📊 Frequency Adverbs — Position

Go before the main verb, but after to be.
✅ I always eat breakfast.  |  She never drinks coffee.
✅ He is usually late. (after is)
ظروف التكرار تأتي قبل الفعل الرئيسي وبعد فعل to be مباشرةً.
