Past Continuous & Past Perfect

🎯 Grade 9 ⏱️ ~40 min الماضي المستمر والماضي التام

🚀 A Scene in the Past

🎬 Picture this: When you arrived home yesterday, your mother was cooking. The food had already been prepared for an hour. And then the lights went out.
Three events. Three different moments in the past. How do we tell the story?

📌 انظر إلى الجملة وحدّد: أيّ الحدثين وقع أولاً في الماضي؟

"When I arrived, she _____ (cook) dinner."
The cooking was already in progress when I arrived.

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form Past Continuous: was / were + verb-ing
  • Form Past Perfect: had + past participle
  • Use when / while / as with Past Continuous
  • Use by the time / before / after / already with Past Perfect
  • Tell a story with layered past events in the right order

📚 The Two Tenses at a Glance

📌 قارِن بين الزمنين — لكل منهما غرض مختلف:
Past Continuous → Action in progress at a moment in the past  (حدث كان جارياً في لحظة ماضية)
Past Perfect → Action completed before another past action  (حدث اكتمل قبل حدث ماضٍ آخر)

🔍 Past Continuous — Spot the Pattern!

📌 لاحظ الكلمة التي تأتي بعد الفاعل في كل جملة — ماذا تلاحظ؟
SubjectSentencePattern
II was reading when she called.was + -ing
He / SheShe was sleeping at midnight.was + -ing
You / We / TheyThey were playing outside.were + -ing
NegativeHe wasn't listening.wasn't + -ing
📌 ما الفرق بين "was" و "were"؟ متى نستخدم كلاً منهما؟

🔍 Past Perfect — Spot the Pattern!

📌 انظر إلى هذه الجمل — ماذا يأتي بعد الفاعل في كل منها؟
SentenceWhich happened first?
When I arrived, she had already left. She left → then I arrived
He was tired because he had worked all day. He worked → then he felt tired
By the time we arrived, the film had started. Film started → then we arrived
She hadn't eaten before she left. She left → she hadn't eaten
📌 ما الصيغة المستخدمة في الماضي التام في كل الجمل؟

🧠 Past Continuous — The Rule

Form: Subject + was / were + verb-ing
الصيغة: الفاعل + was/were + الفعل + ing
UseExample
Action in progress at a specific past timeAt 8pm, I was studying.
Interrupted by a shorter action (when + Past Simple)I was cooking when he arrived.
Two parallel actions (while)She was reading while he was writing.
NegativeThey weren't watching TV.
QuestionWas she sleeping?

🧠 Past Perfect — The Rule

Form: Subject + had + past participle (3rd form) — same for ALL subjects
الصيغة: الفاعل + had + التصريف الثالث — لجميع الفاعلين
UseExample
Action completed BEFORE another past actionWhen she arrived, I had already eaten.
With by the timeBy the time he woke up, we had left.
Reason in the pastHe was tired because he hadn't slept.
NegativeShe hadn't finished before class.
QuestionHad you ever seen snow before?

🧠 When to Use Which?

📌 هذا الجدول يساعدك على اختيار الزمن الصحيح:
SituationTense
Action IN PROGRESS when something else happenedPast Continuous (was/were -ing)
Action COMPLETED before another past actionPast Perfect (had + participle)
The interrupting/second actionPast Simple
Signal words: while, as, at that momentPast Continuous
Signal words: by the time, before, after, alreadyPast Perfect

✏️ Level 1 — Choose the Correct Tense

📌 اختَر الزمن الصحيح لكل جملة — هل الحدث كان جارياً أم اكتمل قبل حدث آخر؟

1. When she called, I _____ (read) a book.

2. By the time we arrived, the match _____ (start).

3. While he _____ (cook), she _____ (set) the table.

✏️ Level 3 — Combine the Events

📌 ادمج الحدثين في جملة واحدة مستخدماً الزمن الصحيح:

1. She left. → Then I arrived. (use: by the time)

2. He studied. + The phone rang (at the same time). (use: while)

3. They didn't eat. → Then the meeting ended. (use: hadn't … before)

🗣️ Tell the Story

📌 اكتب ٤ جمل تصف ما كان يحدث في البيت أمس عند وصولك، مستخدماً الماضي المستمر:

✏️ When you arrived home yesterday, what was everyone doing?

e.g. My sister was doing her homework. My father was reading the newspaper…

🗣️ Before & After — Past Perfect

📌 اكتب ٤ جمل عن أشياء حدثت قبل شيء آخر في الماضي، مستخدماً الماضي التام:

✏️ Write 4 sentences about things that had happened before a past moment.

e.g. By the time I woke up, my mum had already made breakfast. When I arrived at school, the lesson had already started…

📌 تحدٍّ: اكتب فقرة قصيرة (٦ جمل) تصف يوماً لا تنساه، مستخدماً الماضي المستمر والماضي التام معاً:

🏆 Write a short paragraph about a memorable day. Use at least 2 Past Continuous and 2 Past Perfect sentences.
