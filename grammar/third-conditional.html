Third Conditional ⏳

الشرطي الثالث — ماضٍ خيالي لم يحدث

🎯 Grade 9 ⏱️ ~40 min ⏳ If I had… I would have…
🚀 If Only the Past Had Been Different…
We use the Third Conditional to imagine a different PAST — and to talk about regrets.
⏳ Imaginary Past (impossible now)
If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.
…but I didn't study, so I didn't pass. It cannot change now.
الشرطي الثالث يتحدّث عن ماضٍ لم يحدث — نتخيّل نتيجة مختلفة لما مضى، وغالبًا للتعبير عن النَّدم. لنكتشف القاعدة!

📌 Second vs Third — at a Glance
فرّق بين الشرطي الثاني (حاضر خيالي) والشرطي الثالث (ماضٍ خيالي) قبل الاكتشاف.
🟦 Second (imaginary present)
If + past simple → would + base
If I had money, I would buy a car. (now/always)
🟪 Third (imaginary past)
If + past perfect → would have + V3
If I had had money, I would have bought a car. (in the past)
🔍 Spot the Difference!
Look at the verbs. What tense is in the if-clause? What comes after "would"?
لاحظ الأفعال: ما زمن الفعل بعد if؟ وماذا يأتي بعد would؟
Type 2 — imaginary NOW
If I knew [past simple] the answer, I would tell [would + base] you.
About now: I don't know it now.
Type 3 — imaginary PAST
If I had known [past perfect] the answer, I would have told [would have + V3] you.
About the past: I didn't know it then — and it's finished.
💡 The signal: two "haves" = the past.
if-clause: had + V3  ·  main clause: would have + V3.

⭐ The Pattern

🟪 Third: If + had + V3 → would have + V3

🧠 The Full Rule
القاعدة الكاملة للشرطي الثالث مع الأمثلة.
If + past perfect (had + V3),
would have + past participle (V3)
If I had woken up early, I would have caught the bus.
If you had asked me, I would have helped you.
She would have passed if she had revised. (if-clause can come second — no comma)
🧠 Important Notes
⚠️ It is UNREAL & finished: the opposite really happened.
"If I had saved money, I would have bought it." → I didn't save, so I didn't buy. النتيجة الحقيقية دائمًا عكس الجملة — الحدث انتهى ولا يمكن تغييره.
✅ could have / might have can replace would have:
"If he had trained, he could have won." / "…he might have won." could have (كان بإمكانه) و might have (ربّما) بديلان عن would have.
💡 Contractions: I'd have = I would have. Don't write "would of" ✗ — it is "would have" ✓. الاختصار I'd have، والخطأ الشائع "would of" صوابه would have.
