⏱️ Time Expressions — What Changes?
الظروف الزمنية تتغير عند النقل من الحاضر إلى الماضي.
Direct Speech→Reported Speech
now→then
today→that day
tomorrow→the next day / the following day
yesterday→the day before / the previous day
here→there
this→that
these→those
last week→the week before
ago→before

⭐ The Big Pattern

When we report what someone said, the verb goes ONE step back in time.

We call this the "Backshift" — الإزاحة الزمنية

🧠 The Tense Backshift
جدول الإزاحة الزمنية — الأفعال تتحول خطوة نحو الماضي.
Direct Speech Reported Speech
Present Simple
I work here.		 → Past Simple
She said she worked there.
Present Continuous
I am studying.		 → Past Continuous
He said he was studying.
Past Simple
I went out.		 → Past Perfect
She said she had gone out.
will
I will help.		 → would
He said he would help.
can
I can swim.		 → could
She said she could swim.
may
I may come.		 → might
He said he might come.
🧠 Pronouns Change Too
الضمائر تتغير حسب المنظور الجديد.

I → he / she

"I love this."
→ She said she loved that.

we → they

"We are ready."
→ They said they were ready.

my → his / her

"I lost my pen."
→ He said he lost his pen.

you → I / they

"You are late."
→ She told me I was late.

🧠 Reporting Statements vs Questions

Statements

said (that) …
told + person (that) …

"I am tired."
→ She said she was tired.
→ She told me she was tired.

Questions

asked + if/whether (Yes/No)
asked + wh-word (Wh-Q)

"Do you like tea?"
→ He asked if I liked tea.
"Where do you live?"
→ She asked where I lived.

ملاحظة مهمة: عند نقل السؤال، لا نستخدم ترتيب السؤال — الفعل يأتي بعد الفاعل. ✗ She asked where did I live.  |  ✓ She asked where I lived.
