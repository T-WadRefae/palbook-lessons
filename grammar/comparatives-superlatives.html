🧩 Pattern Summary

Short (1–2 syllables): tall → taller → the tallest (+er / +est)
Long (3+ syllables): beautiful → more beautiful → the most beautiful
Irregular: good → better → the best  |  bad → worse → the worst

📝 Spelling rules for short adjectives

• End in consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter → big → bigger
• End in -y: change y → i → heavy → heavier
• End in -e: just add r → nice → nicer

⚠️ Important Usage Rules

✅ Use than after comparatives: "She is taller than me."
✅ Use the before superlatives: "She is the tallest in the class."
❌ NEVER say: "more taller" or "the most tallest" — double forms are wrong!

🇵🇸 Nablus is older than Ramallah.  |  Al-Quds is the most important city.
Palestinian olive oil is better than any other.  |  The Dead Sea is the lowest point on Earth.

🗣️ Speaking Rounds

Round 1 · In our classroom

Compare two objects in your classroom. Use: "___ is ___ than ___."

Round 2 · Animals

Compare two animals: "A ___ is ___ than a ___." Then say which is the ___ (superlative) of all.

Round 3 · School subjects

Tell your partner: "For me, ___ is more difficult than ___. But ___ is the most interesting."

Round 4 · Palestine

Name two Palestinian cities. Say which is bigger, older, or more beautiful. Then name the most famous one!
