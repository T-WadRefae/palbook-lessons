Present Perfect Continuous ⏱️

المضارع التام المستمر — فعلٌ بدأ واستمرّ حتى الآن

🎯 Grade 9 ⏱️ ~40 min ⏱️ have been + -ing
🚀 Still Going… ⏱️
We use the Present Perfect Continuous for an action that started in the past and is STILL happening now.
⏱️ Started before → still now
I have been studying for two hours. (…and I'm still studying!)
المضارع التام المستمر يصف فعلاً بدأ في الماضي وما زال مستمرًّا حتى الآن، ونركّز على مدّته (for/since). لنكتشف الشكل!

📚 Key Vocabulary

📌 for vs since — at a Glance
for للمدّة (كم من الوقت)، و since لنقطة البداية (متى بدأ).
⏳ for = how long (a period)
for two hours · for three days · for a long time
📍 since = starting point
since 2019 · since Monday · since I was ten
🔍 What Do You Notice?
Look at the two helping verbs and the -ing form.
لاحظ الفعلين المساعدين (have/has + been) ثم الفعل بصيغة ing.
Duration — still happening
They have been waiting [have + been + -ing] for an hour.
Started an hour ago — and they are still waiting.
Recent activity — present result
I'm tired because I have been running.
The activity explains why I'm tired NOW.
💡 The shape: subject + have/has been + verb-ing.
Use has been with he/she/it, and have been with I/you/we/they.

⭐ The Pattern

have/has been + verb-ing  ·  for + period  ·  since + start point

🧠 The Full Rule
القاعدة الكاملة للمضارع التام المستمر.
➕ have/has been + verb-ing
➖ haven't/hasn't been + verb-ing
❓ Have/Has + subject + been + verb-ing?
It has been raining all morning.
I haven't been sleeping well lately.
How long have you been waiting?
🧠 Important Notes
⚠️ Focus = the activity & its duration, not the finished number:
"I have been reading this book." (activity, maybe not finished)
"I have read three books." (finished result — present perfect simple) المستمر يركّز على النشاط ومدّته، والبسيط على النتيجة المكتملة.
✅ Great with: for, since, all day, all morning, lately, recently, How long…? يكثر استخدامه مع for / since / all day / How long…؟
💡 Not with stative verbs (know, like, believe): use the simple form.
✗ I have been knowing…  |  ✓ I have known him for years. لا نستخدمه مع أفعال الحالة (know, like) — نستعمل التام البسيط.

🗣️ Speaking — How Long…?
تحدّث عن أنشطة بدأت واستمرّت حتى الآن.
Round 1 — You: 👉 I have been learning English for ___ / since ___ .
Round 2 — Today: 👉 I have been ___ all morning. (studying, helping, playing…)
Round 3 — Results: 👉 I'm tired/hungry/happy because I have been ___ .
Round 4 — Ask a partner: How long have you been …? Ask about their hobby, their school, their town.
