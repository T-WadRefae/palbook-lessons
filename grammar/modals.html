Modal Verbs

🎯 Grades 6 & 7 ⏱️ ~30 min أفعال الأوجه

🚀 What Would You Say?

Your friend looks very worried 😟 — she has a big exam tomorrow!
Which of these would you say to help her?
"You should study!"  ·  "You must sleep early!"  ·  "You can ask the teacher!"
These small words — can, must, should, may — carry huge meaning. They are called modal verbs.

📌 اختَر أفضل نصيحة في كلِّ موقف:

1. Your friend is coughing badly. You say: "You _____ see a doctor."

2. The sign says 🚭 — no smoking. You tell your classmate: "You _____ smoke here."

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Use can for ability and informal permission
  • Use must for strong obligation and prohibition (mustn't)
  • Use should for advice and recommendation
  • Use may for formal permission and possibility
  • Know that modals are always followed by the base verb (no -s / no -ing / no to)

🔍 Same Verb — Different Modal

Read these sentences. Only the modal changes — but the meaning completely changes!

SentenceWhat does it mean?
You can leave early.You are allowed to leave (informal).
You must leave now.It is necessary / required — no choice!
You should leave soon.It is a good idea — my advice.
You may leave now.You are formally permitted to leave.
You mustn't leave.It is forbidden / prohibited!
📌 لاحظ: الفعل leave لم يتغيَّر — الذي تغيَّر هو أداة الوجه (modal) فقط. هل لاحظت أن الفعل بعد أداة الوجه هو دائماً مصدر بسيط (base verb)؟

🧠 The Four Modal Verbs — Full Rules

💪 can

  • Ability
    "She can speak French."
  • Informal permission
    "You can use my phone."
  • Request
    "Can you help me?"
Negative: can't / cannot
"I can't swim."

⚠️ must

  • Strong obligation
    "You must wear a seatbelt."
  • Logical certainty
    "He worked hard — he must be tired."
Negative: mustn't = FORBIDDEN
"You mustn't cheat."

💡 should

  • Advice / recommendation
    "You should eat more fruit."
  • Expectation
    "She should be here by now."
Negative: shouldn't = bad idea
"You shouldn't skip meals."

🎩 may

  • Formal permission
    "May I come in, please?"
  • Possibility
    "It may rain later."
Negative: may not = not allowed (formal)
"You may not enter."

🧠 Form — One Rule for All

TypeStructureExample
✅ PositiveSubject + modal + base verbShe can swim.
❌ NegativeSubject + modal + not + base verbHe must not lie.
❓ QuestionModal + subject + base verb?Should I call you?
⚠️ Golden Rule: After ALL modal verbs → always the base verb
  • ❌ He can to swim → ✅ He can swim
  • ❌ She must studies → ✅ She must study
  • ❌ You should sleeping → ✅ You should sleep

🗣️ Speaking — School Rules & Advice

📌 تحدَّث مع زميلك عن هذه المواقف — استخدم can / must / should / may في كلِّ إجابة:

🏫 Round 1 — School Rules

Tell your partner 3 rules at your school using must and mustn't.

e.g. "Students must be quiet in the library." / "We mustn't use phones in class."

💡 Round 2 — Health Advice

Give your partner 3 pieces of advice for staying healthy using should and shouldn't.

e.g. "You should drink 8 glasses of water a day."

🎩 Round 3 — Formal Requests

Practice asking your teacher for permission using May I…?

e.g. "May I open the window, please?" / "May I go to the bathroom?"
