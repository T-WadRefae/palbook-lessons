Question Tags

🎯 Grades 7 & 9 ⏱️ ~30 min الأسئلة الذيلية

🚀 You Speak Arabic, Don't You?

In everyday conversation, people often add a small question to the end of a sentence to confirm what they think or to invite agreement.
"It's a beautiful day, isn't it?"   "You're from Ramallah, aren't you?"
These endings are called question tags — and they follow a clever pattern!

📌 اختَر الذيل الصحيح لكلِّ جملة:

1. "The weather is nice today, _____?"

2. "She doesn't live in Ramallah, _____?"

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form question tags: positive sentence → negative tag
  • Form question tags: negative sentence → positive tag
  • Use the correct auxiliary verb in the tag
  • Use the correct pronoun in the tag
  • Know special cases: I am…, Let's…, imperatives

🔍 Watch the Pattern!

Look at these sentences carefully. What do you notice about the tag each time?

📝 Example sentences:
"It is cold today, isn't it?"
"They are students, aren't they?"
"She works hard, doesn't she?"
"You went to Hebron, didn't you?"

What did you notice? ✏️

  • The tag uses the same auxiliary as the main clause.
  • If the main clause is positive, the tag is negative.
  • The pronoun in the tag matches the subject.
  • If there's no auxiliary → use do / does / did.

🔍 The Opposite Rule

📌 لاحظ: الجملة الإيجابية ← ذيل سلبي. والجملة السلبية ← ذيل إيجابي:
Main ClauseTagWhy?
She is a doctor, isn't she? Positive → Negative; is → isn't; she → she
They don't like fish, do they? Negative → Positive; don't → do; they → they
You can swim, can't you? Positive → Negative; can → can't; you → you
He hasn't arrived, has he? Negative → Positive; hasn't → has; he → he

🧠 The Question Tag Formula

Positive statement → Negative tag
"She is kind, isn't she?"
Negative statement → Positive tag
"He can't drive, can he?"

📋 How to Build a Tag — 3 Steps

  1. Find the auxiliary verb in the main clause (am/is/are/do/does/did/can/will…)
    If no auxiliary → use do/does (present) or did (past)
  2. Make it opposite (positive → negative / negative → positive)
  3. Add the correct pronoun (matching the subject)

🧠 Full Examples by Tense

Tense / StructureStatementTag
Present SimpleShe likes tea,doesn't she?
Present Simple (neg)They don't work,do they?
Present ContinuousHe is running,isn't he?
Past SimpleYou went home,didn't you?
Past Simple (be)They were late,weren't they?
Present PerfectShe has finished,hasn't she?
Modal (can)You can swim,can't you?
Modal (will)He will come,won't he?
⚠️ Special Cases:
  • "I am late, aren't I?" — NOT "amn't I"
  • "Let's go, shall we?" — suggestion tag
  • "Don't do that, will you?" — imperative tag
  • "Open the door, will you / won't you?" — request tag
📌 حالات خاصة: I am ← aren't I | Let's ← shall we | الأمر ← will you
