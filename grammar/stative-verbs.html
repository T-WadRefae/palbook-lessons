Stative Verbs

🚀 Does This Sound Right?

Your English friend sends you this message:
"Hi! I am knowing the answer to your question. I am loving your idea!"

Something feels wrong… but why? 🤔
The verbs know and love describe states — not actions happening right now. These are called stative verbs, and they have a special rule!

🎯 Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Identify stative verbs (feelings, senses, mental states, possession)

🔍 But Wait — Some Verbs Can Go Both Ways!

📌 بعض الأفعال يمكن أن تكون حالة أو فعلاً ديناميكياً حسب المعنى:

think (stative = opinion)

"I think you are right." ✅
= I believe / my opinion

think (dynamic = mental activity)

"I am thinking about the answer." ✅
= actively processing right now

have (stative = possession)

"I have a new phone." ✅
= I own it

have (dynamic = experience)

"I am having dinner." ✅
= doing the activity now

📌 اختَر الجملة الصحيحة — "think" بمعنى الرأي أم النشاط الذهني؟

"I _____ about your offer." (actively considering right now)

🧠 The Stative Verb Rule

⚡ The Golden Rule

Stative verbs describe a state (not an action in progress).
They are NOT normally used in continuous tenses (-ing forms).

أفعال الحالة تصف وضعاً ثابتاً — لا تُستخدم في الأزمنة المستمرة بصيغة -ing

✅ vs ❌ Quick Examples

"She is knowing French."

→

"She knows French."

"I am wanting a coffee."

→

"I want a coffee."

"He is owning a house."

→

"He owns a house."

🧠 Stative vs Dynamic Summary

Stative (no -ing)Dynamic (can use -ing)
She knows Arabic.She is learning Arabic. (right now)
I love football.I am playing football. (right now)
He has a car.He is having a shower. (activity)
This tastes delicious.She is tasting the soup. (to check)
💡 Quick Test: Can you add "right now" and it still makes sense?
"I know the answer right now" ✅ — but "knowing" sounds wrong → stative!
"She is cooking right now" ✅ — continuous works → dynamic!
📌 اختبار سريع: إذا أضفت "right now" وبدت الجملة طبيعية بالمستمر فالفعل ديناميكي. إذا بدت غريبة فهو فعل حالة!
