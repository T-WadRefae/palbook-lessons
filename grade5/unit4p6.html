🎯 Lesson Aims · أهداف الدرس

To listen and find when we pick each fruit.
أن أستمعَ وأعرفَ متى نقطفُ كلَّ فاكهة.
To read and circle the correct word about harvest months.
أن أقرأَ وأختارَ الكلمةَ الصحيحةَ عن أشهرِ القطاف.
To say the chant about the days in each month.
أن أُنشِدَ نشيدَ عددِ أيامِ الشهور.
To ask and answer: What can you see? When can you pick it?
أن أسألَ وأُجيبَ: «ماذا ترى؟ ومتى تقطفُه؟».

🧺 Key Language

You can collect figs from July to October.
مثال: In August you can start to collect the apples.

📝 Questions · الأسئلة (5)

In March and April.في آذار ونيسان.
2When can you collect figs and grapes?
From July to October.من تمّوز إلى تشرين الأول.
3When can you collect bananas?
In September.في أيلول.
4When do you start to collect apples?
In August.في آب.

In December.في كانون الأول.

🎤 Days in the Months

Thirty days in September, April, June and November.
All the rest have thirty-one,
Except February which has 28 alone
And 29 in a leap year! 🎉
