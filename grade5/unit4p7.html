🎯 Lesson Aims · أهداف الدرس

To write the correct month words (unscramble).
أن أكتبَ أسماءَ الشهورِ صحيحةً (ترتيب الحروف).
To find the months in a wordsearch.
أن أجدَ الشهورَ في شبكةِ الكلمات.
To join two sentences with the word and.
أن أربطَ جملتين بكلمةِ and.
To practise handwriting: the months, month and year.
أن أتدرّبَ على الخطّ: الشهور وmonth وyear.

🔗 Write Correct Sentences

💡

Use the word and to join words together. Don't forget the CAPITAL letter and the full stop (.).

⭐ Model

We collect oranges in December. We collect lemons in December.
We collect oranges and lemons in December.
✍️ اكتبِ الجملةَ المدموجةَ في دفترك، ثم اضغط «Answer» للتحقق.
1In July we collect grapes. In July we collect figs.
In July we collect grapes and figs.
2The summer holidays are in June and July. The summer holidays are in August.
The summer holidays are in June, July and August.
3In August you can start to collect apples. In August you can go swimming.
In August you can start to collect apples and go swimming.
