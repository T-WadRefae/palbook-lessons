GRAMMAR📖 Present Simple — المضارع البسيط

القاعدة كاملةً: التكوين · قواعد إضافة s/es · الاستخدامان (المنتظم / الجدول المستقبلي) · النفي والسؤال · المقارنة مع المضارع المستمر · الأخطاء الشائعة

⚙️ 1. How to form it — كيف نكوّن المضارع البسيط
Subject الضميرVerb الفعلExample مثال
I / You / We / TheytravelThey travel a lot.
He / She / IttravelsHe travels a lot.
📌 The big rule: add -s (or -es) only with he / she / it.   He lives · She flies · The flight leaves.
✏️ 2. Spelling of the he/she/it form — قواعد إضافة s / es
➕ Most verbs → add -s
travel → travels
leave → leaves
get → gets
love → loves
+es after -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o
go → goes
watch → watches
finish → finishes
fix → fixes
consonant + y → -ies
fly → flies
study → studies
carry → carries
try → tries
vowel + y → just +s
play → plays
enjoy → enjoys
stay → stays
say → says
📌 3. Two uses — استخدامان مهمّان
🔁 USE 1 — Regular & permanent العادات والحقائق
لأفعال تحدث بانتظام (عادات/روتين) وللحقائق التي تبقى ثابتة دائماً.
He lives in Palestine.
These tours start from Jerusalem every week.
He loves flying and spends many hours in the air.
⏰ Signal words كلمات دلالية:
alwaysusuallyoftensometimesneverevery weekevery day
🗓️ USE 2 — Future timetables الجداول المستقبلية
لأحداث مستقبلية مُجدوَلة ومثبّتة في برنامج رسمي (رحلات، حافلات، برامج).
Tomorrow, we travel by mini-bus at 9:30.
We begin our walking tour at 10:00.
Flight PF156 takes off at 8:10 tomorrow.
⏰ Signal words كلمات دلالية:
tomorrow at 9:30at 10:00on Mondayleaves atarrives atthe timetable
❓ 4. Negative & Questions — النفي والسؤال
✔ PositiveHe flies to London.  /  They prepare the plane.
✘ Negativeاستخدم doesn't (he/she/it) أو don't (I/you/we/they) + الفعل المجرّد:   He doesn't fly · They don't prepare. (بدون s بعد doesn't!)
? Yes/NoDoes he fly? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.  /  Do they fly? — Yes, they do.
? Wh-What time does the flight leave?  /  When do you arrive in New York?
🔍 5. Present Simple vs. Present Continuous — المقارنة
✅ Present Simple
المضارع البسيط		✅ Present Continuous
المضارع المستمر
MeaningHabits / facts / fixed timetablesHappening now / personal future arrangement
Formverb (+ s/es for he/she/it)am / is / are + verb-ing
ExampleFlight PF156 leaves at 8:10.   He flies every week.Look! The plane is taking off.   I am meeting a friend tomorrow.
Signalsevery day, usually, at 10:00 (timetable)now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
⚠️ 6. Common mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
✘He fly to London.  →  he/she/it needs -s (and fly → flies)!
✔He flies to London.
✘He don't like night flights.  →  use doesn't with he/she/it!
✔He doesn't like night flights.
✘He doesn't likes it.  →  no -s after doesn't!
✔He doesn't like it.
✘The flight is leaving at 8:10 every day.  →  fixed timetable = present simple!
✔The flight leaves at 8:10 every day.
