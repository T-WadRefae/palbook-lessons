💡 Full story

1. Jamie was taking a book back to the library. While he was walking through the park, he saw Waleed, Lana and Lisa.

2. They were giving the ducks some bread, so he put down the book and started doing the same.

3. When they had finished, they went to get an ice cream. He didn't remember the book until he had left the park. He ran back as fast as he could, but the book had gone.

4. When he arrived at the library, he began telling the assistant about the situation. But the assistant smiled and showed him the book — someone had brought it back ten minutes earlier.
