1 We use too … and (not) … enough when we compare things with the right quantity or standard.

نستعملُهما حينَ نقارِنُ شيئًا بالمقدارِ أو المستوى المطلوب.

🧠 The rule in a box — القاعدةُ مُجمَلةً
FormMeaningExample
too + adjective / adverbmore than the right standard The ball is too fast.
not + adjective / adverb + enoughless than the right standard She isn’t quick enough.
+ for + noun / pronounwho it is too much for It’s too high for him.
+ to + infinitivewhat the person cannot do He isn’t strong enough to hold them.

ملحوظة مهمّة: إضافةُ for + اسم/ضمير وإضافةُ المصدرِ to + فعل اختياريّتان، فقد تأتيانِ وقد لا تأتيان: قارِنْ Don’t push yourself too hard. (بلا for) مع Climbing is too dangerous for you. (بـ for).
