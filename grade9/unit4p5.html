Salah Al-Din (1138–1193): a leader ahead of his time

After Salah Al-Din had brought together large parts of the Muslim World, he turned to Jerusalemالقدس. The city had been in crusaderصليبيّ hands all his life, and from 1182 he started preparing to free it from themيعودُ على الصليبيّين.

As a strong believerمؤمن in God and the Prophet (peace be upon him), he believed that he must do this.

He prepared well, and in summer 1187, he defeatedهَزمَ them at the Battle of Hittinمعركة حطّين. Weeks later, Jerusalem fell. For Muslims everywhere, this was a great moment.

It was the opposite in Europe, and a new crusadeحملة صليبيّة began in 1189. But things went badly for the crusaders: by summer 1192, most had died or left Palestine. Only 2,000 men under King Richard of Englandالملك ريتشارد قلب الأسد turned east from the Mediterranean to attack Jerusalem.

Then Richard became sick, and this has given us a famous story. Salah Al-Din did not attack, and instead, he sent Richard fruit, snow to make cool water, and even his personal doctor.

Stories like this show a leader who behavedتصرَّف fairly and generously, as well as one who was brave and intelligent. They show us a leader who was ahead of his time.

Salah Al-Din and Richard never met, but they respectedاحترما each other greatly, and they made peace in 1192. Jerusalem remained in Muslim hands, but people of both religions could travel there freelyبحُرّيّة.

This was almost Salah Al-Din's last important act. He died in Damascus in 1193.

1. Salah Al-Din took Jerusalem before 1182.
From 1182 he started preparing to free Jerusalem; it fell in 1187. بدأَ الاستعدادَ عام ١١٨٢، وفُتِحت القدس عام ١١٨٧.
2. He defeated the crusaders at the Battle of Hittin in 1187.
✔️ Correct — Hittin, summer 1187. صحيح — في معركةِ حطّين صيفَ ١١٨٧.
3. Salah Al-Din attacked King Richard when he became sick.
He did not attack — he sent Richard fruit, snow and his own doctor. لم يُهاجِمْه؛ بل أرسلَ له الفاكهةَ والثلجَ وطبيبَه.
4. After 1192, people of both religions could travel to Jerusalem freely.
✔️ Correct — Jerusalem stayed Muslim, but open to all. القدس بقيت للمسلمين، وفُتِحت لزيارةِ الجميع.
5. Salah Al-Din died in Damascus in 1193.
✔️ Correct. صحيح — تُوُفّي في دمشقَ عام ١١٩٣.
