📘 Past simple passive: was / were + past participle

ActivePassive
Our ancestors cut this stone.This stone was cut by our ancestors. ✔ useful
People built the cities with stone.The cities were built with stone. by people ✘

نصوغُه من was / were + التصريفِ الثالث. نذكرُ الفاعلَ بعدَ by فقط إذا أعطانا معلومةً مفيدة، ونحذفُه إذا كان عامًّا مثلَ people / they / someone.

💡 Example answer

For a very long time, a new school was needed by our little town, and finally, last year, it was built by our community. Two years ago, some land was bought by a group of parents, and a very good architect was invited to design the school by the same group. In the next few months, some excellent plans were drawn by the architect, and enough money to pay for the project was given by the government. Top-quality local stone was chosen by the architect, and the building work was given to the best builders in the area by the local group. At the end, all the new pupils and their parents were invited to a big party by the school, and the party was reported by the local newspaper on its front page the next day!
