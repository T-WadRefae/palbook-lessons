📖 Prepositions Reminder Guide

📍 IN
✅ Use 1: Performance / how well you do in something How well did you do in the test?
✅ Use 2: Future time (months, years, weeks) Where will we be in six months?
⏳ FOR
✅ Use 1: Waiting / purpose We have to wait for the bus.
✅ Use 2: Duration of time I studied for two hours.
🤝 WITH
✅ Use 1: Accompaniment / together I like being with other people.
✅ Use 2: Using a tool She wrote with a pen.
💭 ABOUT
✅ Use 1: Feelings / opinions on a topic I'm not happy about the new plans.
📅 ON
✅ Use 1: Days of the week / specific dates What are you doing on Friday?
