📄 The whole paragraph — الفقرةُ كاملةً
Checking heart rates and fitness. The tests on Steve and Kate produced the following information. Before exercise, Steve’s resting heart rate was 73 beats a minute, but Kate’s was 65. Then, during exercise, Steve’s heart rate rose quickly to 155 beats a minute. However, Kate’s rose less, and less quickly, to 132. After exercise, Steve’s rate fell very slowly to its resting rate after 3 minutes 20 seconds, but Kate’s rate fell much faster, after only 1 minute 30 seconds.
