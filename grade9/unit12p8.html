Connectors of Cause & Result

🎯 Learning Objectives · أهداف الدرس
  • 1
    Identify and use connectors of cause: because, since, as
    أتعرّف على أدوات ربط السبب وأستخدمها بشكل صحيح
  • 2
    Use the connector of result: so
    أستخدم "so" للتعبير عن النتيجة

    Use because of that to refer back to a previous cause
    أستخدم "because of that" للإشارة لسبب ذُكر سابقاً

    Understand the position of cause & result in a sentence
    أفهم ترتيب السبب والنتيجة داخل الجملة

    Rewrite sentences using Because of that
    أعيد كتابة الجمل باستخدام "Because of that"

🔄 Activity 3 · Use Because of that
غيّر الجملة الثانية باستخدام "Because of that" — ادمج الفكرتين بجملتين مترابطتين 🔗
1
I could see dark rain clouds. As the weather was looking bad, I took my umbrella.
✦ I could see dark rain clouds. Because of that, I took my umbrella.
(Example – already done)
🧩
2
I worked hard to get ready for the test. I got good marks since I had prepared well.
3
The trip takes four hours. Because it's a long way, we're taking a picnic lunch.
4
My sister is using our computer. I can't go online as she's using it.
