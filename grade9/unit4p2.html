📖 Activity 3: Read again and answer the questions.

❓ 1 Who got on the bus first?
Jamie got on first.جامي صعدَ أوّلًا، ثمَّ لحقَه وليد.
❓ 2 What was Waleed doing yesterday evening?
He was trying to phone Jamie.كان يحاولُ أن يتَّصلَ بجامي.
❓ 3 Why couldn't Waleed and Jamie talk yesterday evening?
Jamie was out — he was getting books from the library.لأنَّ جامي كان خارجَ البيت، يُحضِرُ كتبًا من المكتبة.
❓ 4 What is the subject of the new school project?
"A great leader".المشروع عن قائدٍ عظيم.
❓ 5 What two things do they have to think about?
Personal qualities and what the person did.الصفاتُ الشخصيّة، وما فعلَه الشخصُ في حياتِه.
❓ 6 Did Jamie only find a book about Alexander the Great?
No, he also noticed a book about Salah Al-Din.لا؛ لاحظَ أيضًا كتابًا عن صلاحِ الدين.
❓ 7 Why did the book remind Jamie of Salah Al-Din?
Because he saw a TV documentary about him last week.لأنَّه شاهدَ فيلمًا وثائقيًّا عنه الأسبوعَ الماضي.
❓ 8 Why does Jamie choose Salah Al-Din for his project?
Because he tried to be fair and generous to everyone — even his enemies.لأنَّه حاولَ أن يكونَ عادلًا وكريمًا مع الجميع، حتّى أعدائِه.
