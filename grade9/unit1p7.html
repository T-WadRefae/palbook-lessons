STEP 3📖 Activity 3: Find the correct meanings

اقرأ مدخل القاموس للكلمة، ثم لكل جملة اختر رقم المعنى الصحيح (1 أو 2 أو 3) للكلمة المُسطّرة.

centre (noun)
1
the middle of an area: We stood in the centre of the room.
2
the part of a town/city with most shops and restaurants: We took a bus to the centre.
3
a place which is important for something: Hollywood is the centre of the film world.
great (adjective)
1
bigger or more than is usual: The birds flew south in great numbers.
2
important: Oum Kalthoum was a great singer.
3
very good or nice: You look great in that new coat.
