Your visit to Istanbul

We think you should visit two important places in town – the Blue Mosque in Sultanahmet and Aya Sofya, which is very close to it. We also think you should spend a little time at a coffee shop in Sultanahmet Park, between the two places. You could try a little Turkish tea and a few biscuits.

We think that Sultanahmet Park is a good place to start your tour. From there, turn left and go to the great Blue Mosque. When you leave, go back across the gardens and stop at the coffee shop. Then go straight on to Aya Sofya. It's only a short walk, so it's easy for everyone.
