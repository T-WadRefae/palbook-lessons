GATE 1

Lesson Objectives

What you will learn in this airport adventure ✈️

📘

New Vocabulary

Learn 9 key travel words: airport, attendant, captain, complete, land, landing card, passport, right now, take off.

تعلّم 9 كلمات سفر جديدة عن المطار والرحلات الجوية.
🖼️

Describe Pictures

Look at airport scenes and describe what people are doing using full English sentences.

وصف الصور باستخدام جمل كاملة عن الأشخاص في المطار.
🎧

Listening Skills

Listen to a story about the Yafawi family travelling to Palestine and answer comprehension questions.

الاستماع إلى قصة عائلة اليافاوي والإجابة عن أسئلة الفهم.
💬

Reading Dialogues

Read and understand conversations between travellers, attendants, captains and family members.

قراءة وفهم المحادثات بين المسافرين وأفراد طاقم الطائرة والعائلة.
🗣️

Everyday English

Use polite welcoming phrases like "It's lovely to have you here" and "It's lovely to be here".

استخدام عبارات الترحيب اليومية للتعبير عن السعادة.
🇵🇸

Cultural Connection

Discover how Palestinian-American families return home and connect with their heritage.

اكتشاف كيفية عودة العائلات الفلسطينية الأمريكية إلى وطنها.
📍

Scene 1 · Taking Off

The Yafawi family begins their journey to Palestine

📖
Narrator
The Yafawi family usually have their summer holidays in America, but this year Hadeel and Nidal are having a summer holiday with a very big difference. It is 10:30 in the morning on Saturday, July 10th, and they are taking off on a long flight — Flight PF124 to Gaza. Soon after that, they hear the captain.
