🎧 CD1 Audio 2 · The Yafawi family conversation
كلمة جديدة 🟡 ضمير 🟣
🛫 Taking off — Flight PF124 to Gaza
NarratorThe Yafawi family usually have their summer holidays in America, but this year Hadeel and Nidal are having a summer holiday with a very big difference. It is 10:30 in the morning on Saturday, July 10th, and they are taking off on a long flight — Flight PF124 to Gaza. Soon after that, they hear the captain.
CaptainWe're now flying at 11,500 metres, and the weather ahead is excellent.
NidalThis is exciting!
HadeelPalestine, here we come!
📋 Comprehension (1) سؤال الفهم — الإقلاع▼
💁 During the long flight — the attendant
AttendantAre you from Palestine?
HadeelWell, we're Palestinian-American. We're staying with our cousin's family when we get there. We're staying with them for four weeks.
NidalWe're going home in early August.
AttendantSo you each need to complete a visitor landing card. They'll take it after you arrive, when you show them your passport. Here you are.
📋 Comprehension (2) أسئلة الفهم — على متن الطائرة▼
🛬 At Gaza Airport, next morning
NarratorAt Gaza Airport next morning, the family is waiting:
RamiI can't wait to see them again.
Uncle BasimWell, they're arriving at 9:30.
Aunt NadaAnd it's almost 9:30 now, so they're coming very soon.
RamiNo, they're landing right now. That's their plane!
📋 Comprehension (1) سؤال الفهم — في مطار غزّة▼
🤗 45 minutes later — a warm welcome
RamiNidal! Hadeel! Hi!
NidalHi, Rami!
HadeelIt's great to see you again!
Uncle BasimHello, Hadeel. Hello, Nidal. Welcome home to Palestine.
Aunt NadaIt's lovely to have you here.
HadeelThank you, Uncle Basim, Aunt Nada. It's lovely to be here.
