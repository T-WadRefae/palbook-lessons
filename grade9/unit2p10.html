A School Creed

a poem about our school
1This is our school.
2Let peace dwell here,
3Let the rooms be full of contentment,
4Let love abide here,
5Love of one another,
6Love of mankind,
7Love of life itself,
8And love of God.
9Let us remember
10That, as many hands build a house,
11So many hearts make a school.
📖 Difficult words — كلماتٌ صعبة
abide = livecontentment = happiness creed = a set of beliefsdwell = live let = to make something possiblemankind = the human race

Listen again · Decide the meanings

ثلاثُ كلماتٍ من القصيدة. اختَرِ المعنى الصحيحَ لكلٍّ منها.

Listen again · Find the lines

اضغطْ على الزرِّ لترى الأسطرَ المقصودةَ مُظلَّلةً في القصيدةِ أعلاه.

Read the poem to a partner

اقرأِ القصيدةَ لزميلِك مراعيًا الإيقاعَ والوقف، ثمَّ تبادلا الدَّور.
