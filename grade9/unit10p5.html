Will the Oceans Live or Die?

1
Fish and many other kinds of life, like coralالمرجان – كائنات بحرية تشكّل الشعاب, are disappearingتختفي – تصبح نادرة أو تنتهي from the oceans fast. There are sad changes everywhere. Near the coast, they are often caused by pollutionالتلوّث – تلوّث الماء بمواد ضارة. Farther out, the cause is often over-fishingالصيد الجائر – الصيد أكثر مما تحتمله الطبيعة.   • What are the two main causes of damage to sea life mentioned in paragraph 1?
  • What does "man-made disaster" mean? Give an example from the text.
  • Why are fishing boats described as dangerous? Use evidence from the text.
  • What does the word "disappearing" suggest about the speed of change?

  • Why were whales being hunted two centuries ago? Mention TWO reasons.
  • How did modern technology change whale hunting by the 1940s?
  • What does "numbers were collapsing" mean? Why is this phrase effective?
  • What grammar tense is used throughout this paragraph? Why?
  • Find a phrase that means whales had no protection at all.

Finally, the world took action. In 1985, almost every countryتقريباً كل دولة – الغالبية العظمى من دول العالم agreed to stop catching whales. This means that whale populationsأعداد السكان / التعداد – عدد الأفراد في مجموعة are slowly risingترتفع – تزداد وتتحسّن again.
  • What action did the world take in 1985?
  • Why does the writer use the word "finally"? What does it imply?
  • How is this paragraph different in tone from paragraph 2?

  • Why does the writer believe fish cannot be saved like whales?
  • What is different about fishing rules in Europe compared to the rest of the world?
  • What does "almost certainly not" tell us about the writer's opinion?
  • Is the writer hopeful or pessimistic in this paragraph? Justify your answer.

  • What solution does the writer suggest for saving the oceans?
  • Why does the writer call farming an "ancestors' discovery"?
  • What objection do people have to fish farms? How does the writer respond?
  • Do you agree with the writer's conclusion? Give your own opinion.

they يعود على / refers to "sad changes" — التغيّرات المؤسفة التي حدثت للحياة البحرية المذكورة قبلها. / The sad changes to sea life mentioned in the previous sentence.
Line 2
Farther out, the cause …
Farther out = أبعد عن الساحل / away from the coast — في أعماق المحيط بعيداً عن الشاطئ. / In the deeper parts of the ocean, further from the shore.

… the job was being made easier …
the job يعود على / refers to catching whales — مهمة اصطياد الحيتان التي أصبحت أسهل بفضل التكنولوجيا الحديثة في الأربعينيات. / The work of hunting whales, which became easier with modern technology in the 1940s.
Line 12
Sadly, almost certainly not.
almost certainly not = على الأرجح لا / fish and fishing cannot be helped in the same way — الكاتب لا يعتقد أن ما نجح مع الحيتان يمكن تطبيقه على الأسماك لأن الكثير من الناس يعتمدون عليها غذاءً. / The writer does not believe that a global ban can work for fish, because too many people depend on them for food.
📌 Task 2 – Say what the underlined phrases mean
Line 3
…, the cause is often over-fishing.
Over-fishing means taking too many fish from the sea — catching fish faster than they can reproduce, so numbers keep falling and species may disappear.الصيد الجائر يعني أخذ كميات كبيرة جدًا من الأسماك من البحر - صيد الأسماك بمعدل أسرع من قدرتها على التكاثر، لذلك تستمر أعدادها في الانخفاض وقد تختفي الأنواع
Line 4
We risk the death of the oceans.
The death of the oceans means the complete destruction of all ocean life — a future where fish, coral, and all sea creatures have disappeared because of human damage and pollution. التدمير الكامل للحياة البحرية بأكملها - مستقبل تختفي فيه الأسماك والشعاب المرجانية وجميع الكائنات البحرية بسبب الأضرار البشرية والتلوث
Line 8
… and numbers were collapsing.
Were collapsing means were falling very fast and dangerously — whale populations were dropping so sharply that whole species were close to extinction.كانت أعداد الحيتان تتناقص بسرعة كبيرة وبشكل خطير - فقد انخفضت أعدادها بشكل حاد لدرجة أن أنواعًا كاملة كانت على وشك الانقراض.
Line 10
Finally, the world took action.
The world took action means countries around the world did something to solve the problem — in 1985, almost every country agreed to stop catching whales to save them from extinction.قامت دول العالم بجهود لحل المشكلة - ففي عام 1985، وافقت جميع الدول تقريباً على التوقف عن صيد الحيتان لإنقاذها من الانقراض.
