📌 Grammar focus — Present continuous (am/is/are + verb-ing):
⏱️
NOW for actions happening at this moment / around now — We are flying at 11,500 metres.
🗓️
FUTURE for fixed plans & arrangements — They are arriving in Gaza tomorrow.
