By the end of this lesson, you will be able to...

  • 👀
    Recognize the modal verbs may and might and know they have the same meaning. يتعرف الطالب على may و might ويدرك أنهما يحملان المعنى نفسه.
  • ✍️
    Use may / might + base verb to talk about possibility. يستخدم may / might متبوعَين بالفعل المجرد للتعبير عن الاحتمال.

📘 The Grammar Rules

💡

1. What do they mean?

We use may and might to say something is possible.
We're not 100% sure! 🤔

نستخدم may و might للتعبير عن أن شيئًا ما محتمل الحدوث. كلاهما يعني "ربما".
☁️ It may rain tomorrow. = It might rain tomorrow.
(ربما تمطر غدًا — لا نعرف بالتأكيد)
🧩

يأتي بعد may / might دائمًا الفعل المجرد (بدون s ، بدون to ، بدون ed).
❌ She may comes ✅ She may come
🚫

3. The Negative Form

may not = might not = ربما لا

He may not come with us. = He might not come with us.
✦ نضع not بعد may / might مباشرة.
✦ لا نستخدم don't أو doesn't مع may / might.
❌ He doesn't may come ✅ He may not come
🔄

4. Replacing Possibility Words

We can rewrite sentences with these words:

Old Word
New Form
Maybe / Perhaps
may / might
It's possible
may / might
Before: Maybe Fatima isn't feeling well.
After: Fatima may not be feeling well.
⚠ عند إضافة may / might نحذف will وأفعال الكينونة (am/is/are/won't) ونستخدم الفعل المجرد.
