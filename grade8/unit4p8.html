Advice & suggestions

النصيحةُ والاقتراح 🪧

أربعُ طرقٍ نقولُ بها «افعلْ هذا» و«ماذا لو…» — فلنتعلَّمِ الفرقَ بينَها.

You had better rest.
You had better not go to school.
You could start a new sport.
What about walking for an hour?

🎯 Lesson Objectivesأهداف الدرس

🔍

Discover

Look at four sentences and work out the pattern. تأمَّلْ أربعَ جُمَلٍ واستنتِجْ القاعدة.

🧠

The Rule

Use had better for advice; could and What about…ing for ideas. had better للنصيحة؛ could و What about للاقتراح.

✅

Advice

Use You'd better or You'd better not. تدرَّبْ على استعمالِ الصيغةِ المناسبة.

💡

Suggestions

Use You could… or What about…? قدِّمِ الاقتراحَ بإحدى الصيغتَين.

🧪

Mix

Pick the right form in six quick exercises. اختَرِ الصيغةَ الصحيحةَ في ستّةِ تمارينَ سريعة.

💚

Value

The right word softens a hard truth. الكلمةُ المناسبةُ تُلطِّفُ الحقيقةَ القاسية.

🔍 Discover the patternاكتشِفِ القاعدة

🔍 Activity 1: Read sentences 1–4. Then choose the best way to complete the statements.
تأمَّلْ شكلَ الفعلِ بعدَ كلِّ صيغة، ثمَّ اختَرِ الإكمالَ الصحيح.

1 You had better do our quiz.

2 You had better not do this.

3 You could start a new sport.

4 What about walking for an hour?

1The speaker thinks it will be good for the listener to do or not do something
2In 3–4, the speaker is giving an idea; in 1–2 the speaker is giving .
3In sentences 3–4, the bold words are ways of .
4We can change You could start a new sport? to What about a new sport? بعد What about يأتي الفعلُ بصيغةِ ing.
🧠 The patterns
You had better + verb  ·  You had better not + verb
You could + verb  ·  What about + verb-ing? النصيحةُ أقوى (قد يسوءُ شيءٌ إن لم يُفعَل)، والاقتراحُ فكرةٌ من فكرتَين.

✅ Give advicehad better · had better not

✅ Activity 2: Add You had (You'd) better… or You had (You'd) better not…
اقرأِ الموقفَ ثمَّ اختَرِ الصيغةَ الصحيحةَ لإعطاءِ النصيحة.
1I feel really ill. go to school today. المرضُ يعني ألّا تذهبَ — ننفي النصيحة.
2I have a bad cough. take this medicine. نُثبِتُ النصيحةَ لأنَّ الدواءَ ضروريّ.
3I'm very tired. stay in bed.
4I'm not well. try to do anything today.

💡 Make suggestionscould · What about …ing?

💡 Activity 3: Add suggestions. Use the words in brackets with You could… and What about…?
كوِّنْ الاقتراحَ بالطريقتَين: You could + فعل، وWhat about + فعلٌ بصيغةِ ing.

🍽️ Food shopping

Deema: What should we have for dinner?
You could get some chicken, or what about having some burgers?
يُمكِنُكم اقتراحُ الدجاج، أو «ما رأيُك بالبرغر؟».
Deema: I want some fruit, but what kind should I get?
You could get some apples, or what about getting a melon?
تفّاح، أو «ما رأيُك بالشمّام؟».

💪 Getting fit and healthy

Fuad: The doctor says I should do more exercise. How?
You could go swimming, or what about going running?
السباحة، أو «ما رأيُك بالجري؟».
Fuad: I need to find out more about healthy living. How?
You could read this book, or what about going on the Internet?
اقرأِ هذا الكتاب، أو «ما رأيُك بالبحثِ على الإنترنت؟».
🧠 Remember
You could walk  ·  What about walking? مع could الفعلُ مجرَّد، ومع What about الفعلُ بصيغةِ ing.
