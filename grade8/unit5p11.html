📝 Activity 2: Write about the cousins’ training.
١) أكملِ الجملةَ الأولى بكلمةٍ من جدولِ الحصّةِ التاسعة (their / they). ٢) أكملْ بقيّةَ الفقرةِ بأدواتِ التسلسلِ من الصندوق.

📦 Word box

Adnan and Omar went to their final training the evening before ______ big match, and ______ had a lot to do. ______, they started with 20 minutes of exercises. ______ they practised passing the ball. ______ they practised kicking the ball down the field. ______ they ran round the pitch three times. ______ they practised running with the ball. ______ they played a game for 20 minutes.
💡 First starts the list and Finally ends it. In the middle, use Then, Next or After that.تبدأُ القائمةُ بـ First وتنتهي بـ Finally، وفي الوسطِ نستعملُ Then أو Next أو After that.

🏃‍♀️ Paragraph 2الفقرةُ الثانية: تدريبُ ياسمين

🏃‍♀️ Activity 3: Write the rest of paragraph 2.
أكملِ الجملةَ الأولى بـ hers / her / she، ثمَّ اكتبْ بقيّةَ الفقرةِ مستعملًا أدواتِ التسلسلِ والأنشطةَ التي اخترتَها لياسمين في جدولِ الحصّةِ العاشرة.

📦 Word box

Yasmeen went to ______ the evening before ______ big match, too, and ______ also had a lot to do.
🔗 3+ sequencers👩 uses she✏️ 5+ sentences⏮️ past tense (-ed, ran…)
👀 Model paragraph
Yasmeen went to hers the evening before her big match, too, and she also had a lot to do. First, she started with ten minutes of exercises. Then she practised passing the ball. Next, she practised running with the ball. After that, she stopped to have a rest. Finally, she played a game for 20 minutes.هذه فقرةٌ نموذجيّة؛ فقرتُك قد تختلفُ بحسبِ الأنشطةِ التي اخترتَها.
