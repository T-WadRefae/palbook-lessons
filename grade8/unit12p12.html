  • 4Read and present the group's letter to the class.
✈️ Unit Task
Write a formal letter to Ms Sally Green, Personal Assistant to Dr Cook at Fairfax High, responding to her request for personal details and parents' questions about the visit.

اضغط على كل جزء من أجزاء الرسالة لتظهر لك الشرح والتعليمات 👇

▶ ① Your Address — عنوانك أنت
Surda Basic Mixed School,
Birzeit, Palestine.
5th May, 2025
Top RIGHT of the page · Include the date below

اكتب عنوانك الخاص (عنوان مدرستك) في أعلى يمين الورقة، ثم ضع التاريخ تحته مباشرةً. استخدم الأعداد الترتيبية للتاريخ: 1st، 2nd، 3rd…

▶ ② Receiver's Address — عنوان المُرسَل إليه
Ms Sally Green,
Personal Assistant to Dr Cook,
Fairfax High School, [Address]
Top LEFT · Below your address

اكتب اسم الشخص الذي تُرسل إليه وعنوانه الكامل في أعلى يسار الورقة، تحت عنوانك أنت.

▶ ③ Greeting — التحية
Dear Ms Green,
Formal greeting — use title + surname

نعرف اسم الشخص، لذلك نكتب Dear Ms Green, — استخدم دائماً اللقب (Mr / Ms / Dr) مع اسم العائلة فقط.

▶ ④ Subject Line — سطر الموضوع
Re: School Trip to Fairfax School
Underline it · Keep it short and clear

سطر الموضوع يُخبر القارئ بموضوع الرسالة قبل أن يقرأها. اجعله قصيراً وواضحاً، وضع تحته خطاً أو اكتبه بخط غامق.

▶ ⑤ Paragraph 1 — الفقرة الأولى (الافتتاحية)
It was good to receive your letter, and we are very happy that our group has been invited to visit Fairfax High School.
Friendly but formal · Refer to their letter

ابدأ بجملة مهذبة تُشير إلى الرسالة التي استلمتها. استخدم النموذج: "It was good to receive … and we are very happy that …"

▶ ⑥ Paragraph 2 — التفاصيل الشخصية
Here are the personal details of everyone in our group.

[أضف الجدول: الاسم · تاريخ الميلاد · الهاتف · العنوان]
Start with the exact opening sentence · Then attach the table

ابدأ بالجملة كما هي بالضبط، ثم ضع جدول المعلومات الذي أكملته مع مجموعتك. تأكد أن جميع البيانات صحيحة وكاملة.

▶ ⑦ Paragraph 3 — أسئلة أولياء الأمور
Here are some important questions from our parents. [Tariq's] father asks where past groups of student visitors have come from…
Use indirect speech · Each student adds their question

استخدم الكلام غير المباشر (reported speech): [Name]'s father asks … / mother wants to know … — كل طالب يضيف السؤال الذي اختاره من قائمة الكتاب.

▶ ⑧ Closing Sentence — جملة الختام
We hope to hear from you soon.
Final sentence of the body

هذه الجملة تُنهي متن الرسالة بأسلوب مهذب وتُشير إلى أنك تنتظر الرد.

▶ ⑨ Formal Ending — الإنهاء الرسمي
Yours sincerely,

[Your Full Name]
Yours sincerely → when you KNOW the name · Yours faithfully → when you DON'T

بما أننا نعرف اسم Ms Green نكتب Yours sincerely, ثم اسمك الكامل. لا تكتب المسمى الوظيفي (Job line).

📌 Yours sincerely ← تعرف الاسم  |  Yours faithfully ← لا تعرف الاسم
✍️ Activity 1 — Work in Groups
  • 1
    Read what she wants · underline key words Read Sally Green's letter. Underline the key words in each paragraph.
  • 2
    Form groups Arrange pupils in groups of four or five.
  • 3
    Draw a table — page 53 Copy the personal details table from Period 2 into your notebook. Fill it in with names, dates of birth, phone numbers and addresses.
  • 4
    Start your formal letter Write your school address on the right, Fairfax High's address on the left.
  • 5
    Greeting Add the greeting: Dear Ms Green,
  • 6
    Subject line Agree on a subject line — e.g. School Trip to Fairfax School.
  • 7
    Paragraph 1 — Opening Write the first paragraph using the model:
    It was good to receive … , and we are very happy that …
  • 8
    Paragraph 2 — Personal details Start with the sentence below, then add the table:
    Here are the personal details of everyone in our group.
  • 9
    Read the questions — choose one from your parents Each pupil picks one question from the list below.
  • 10
    Report to the group Use the model: My parents want to know …
  • 11
    Paragraph 3 — Parents' questions Add each question using the suggested beginning:
    Here are some important questions from our parents. (Tariq's) father asks …
  • 12
    Finish the letter Add: We hope to hear from you soon.
  • 13
    Formal ending Write: Yours sincerely, [Full Name] — do NOT write a Job line.
  • 14
    Present Choose a pupil from each group to read the letter to the class.

🗣️ Report Model

How to report a question:
My parents ask  (what / when / where / who / how / how often …)
My parents want to know  …

✦ My parents want to know where past groups of student visitors have come from.
✦ [Tariq's] father asks how often they will allow our sons to call home.
