🎯Lesson Objectives · أهداف الدرس

  • 📖
    Learn new phrasal verbs about housework and daily activities. تعلّم أفعال مركّبة جديدة تتعلق بالأعمال المنزلية والأنشطة اليومية.
  • 🎧
    Listen to and understand a phone conversation between two friends. الاستماع إلى محادثة هاتفية بين صديقين وفهمها.
  • 🕌
    Discover when Islam came to Spain and explore Islamic Spain. اكتشاف وقت وصول الإسلام إلى إسبانيا واستكشاف الأندلس.
  • 💬
    Practise everyday English expressions like "Is anything wrong?" and "Poor you!". التدرّب على عبارات إنجليزية يومية للتعاطف والاستفسار.

    Build sentences describing a picture using context clues. بناء جمل لوصف صورة باستخدام السياق.

💬The Conversation · المحادثة

Activity 4: Listen and read. Find the words and phrases from Activity 1.

▶ Play Audio ▶ Watch Video
📖 NarratorMike is working hard when Sami calls. He puts down his pen and picks up his phone.
🟢 SamiHi, Mike? Is anything wrong? You didn't play football with us after school.
🔴 MikeI couldn't. I've got lots of homework.
🟢 SamiWhat about doing that tomorrow? It's Saturday.
🔴 MikeNo, I want to hurry up and finish today if I can.
🟢 SamiBut why?
🔴 MikeWe've got some visitors tomorrow, so I have to get up early and help do the housework. I have to tidy up my room, sweep up round the house — and help with the guests, too!
🟢 SamiPoor you! So what are you working on now?
🔴 MikeMy project about Islamic Spain.
🟢 SamiOh, yes, when Muslims from North Africa took Spain.
🔴 MikeYes, they took over the country, and I need to find out the date.
🟢 SamiI can help. I'll look up my History of Islam here.
🔴 MikeOh, thanks!
🟢 SamiI'm looking for the right place. Islamic art … Islamic culture … Ah! Islamic Spain!
🔴 MikeWell done!
🟢 SamiHere it is. Listen, the Muslims arrived in the year 711, and they stayed for almost 800 years.
🔴 MikeThanks, Sami!
🟢 SamiAnd now I think you should turn off your computer and stop. It's late!
