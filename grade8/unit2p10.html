📌 Lesson Objectives

🎧 Listen to a poem and find its general idea.

🤫 Notice the quiet sounds and the loud sounds in the poem.

📜 Read the poem aloud with good rhythm.

🎧 CD1 Audio 20 · Music … is everywhere

Music …

BY ANN BONNER
QUIET SOUNDS · أصواتٌ هادئة
Music …
is everywhere.
In the birds of the air.
In the hum of the honeybee.
In the song of the breeze
as it shivers the trees.
In the river that murmurs
over the stones.
LOUD SOUNDS · أصواتٌ عالية
In the snow wind that moans.
In the surge of the sea
lapping the shore.
In the roar of the storm
rattling the door.
In the drum of the rain
on the windowpane.
Music is here.
Filling your ear.
