📖 Grammar – Reported Wh- Questions
📌 What is a Reported Question?

A reported question tells us what someone asked. We do NOT use a question mark (?) and we change the word order to statement form.

نقل ما سأله شخص ما — بدون علامة استفهام وبترتيب الجملة الخبرية
Direct Question Reported Question
'What does my name mean?' He asks what his name means.
'Where were my family from?' He asks where his family were from.
'When did people start using family names?' Tina wants to know when people started using family names.
'When will you have time?' Rose wants to know when she will have time.
📐 How to Change – 5 Steps
1
Use a reporting verb: asks / wants to know / needs to check
استخدم فعل التبليغ المناسب
2
Keep the Wh- word: what / where / when / how
احتفظ بكلمة السؤال كما هي
3
Change word order → subject + verb (NOT verb + subject)
الفاعل أولاً ثم الفعل — عكس السؤال المباشر
4
Remove the helping verb (did / will) and change the main verb form
احذف الفعل المساعد وغيّر صيغة الفعل الرئيسي
5
End with a full stop (.) — NOT a question mark (?)
أنهِ بنقطة (.) وليس علامة استفهام
