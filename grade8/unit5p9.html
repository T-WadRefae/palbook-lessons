🎯 Lesson Objectivesأهداف الدرس

🔑

Possessives

Use my, your, his, her, our, their and mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. استعمالُ صفاتِ الملكيّةِ وضمائرِها.

👥

some- / every-

Complete a table and a conversation with someone, everything… إكمالُ جدولٍ وحوارٍ بكلماتِ some وevery.

🤝

Agree

Answer statements with too or either. الردُّ على الجملِ بـ too أو either.

💛

Value

Look after your things – and help others find theirs. نحافظُ على أغراضِنا ونساعدُ غيرَنا في إيجادِ أغراضِهم.

The Al-Masri brothers and sisters have prepared a picnic for their school team’s match at another school. Now they are waiting for the team’s bus to arrive.

Deema Fuad, Hanan, Sadiq! Listen, (1) ______! Have we got (2) ______ for our picnic? For example, where’s the bread?

Sadiq I think I’ve seen it (3) ______. Maybe it’s in this bag … Yes, it is.

Deema Good, but I can’t see the apples anywhere.

Fuad Yes, I’ve been looking for them (4) ______, too, and I haven’t seen them anywhere.

Deema Well, can (5) ______ run back to the house and look for them?

Hanan Yes, I’ll do that.

Fuad Oh, no! Now there’s (6) ______ else to worry about.

Deema What’s that?

Fuad The bus is coming! Hanan had better be very quick or she’ll miss it!

🤝 Me too! / Me neither!قلْ جملًا مثبتةً ومنفيّة، وردَّ بـ too أو either

🤝 Activity 3: Work in pairs. Make positive and negative statements. Answer with too or either.
اخترِ الردَّ الذي يوافقُ الجملة، فيظهرُ ردٌّ آخرُ يخالفُها. اضغطْ 🔊 وتدرّبْ مع زميلِك.
1A I love table tennis. B I do, too. OR: I don’t. I prefer (normal tennis).
2A I’ve never been to a basketball match. B I haven’t, either. OR: I have. I’ve been to (several games).
3A I don’t like waking up early.
🙋 OR (disagree): I do. I love the morning!
4A I’ve lost my pens and pencils.
🙋 OR (disagree): I haven’t. Mine are in my bag.
5A I’m trying to get fit at the moment.
🙋 OR (disagree): I’m not. I’m very busy at the moment.
6A I didn’t watch any TV yesterday.
🙋 OR (disagree): I did. I watched a football match.
7A I was doing homework all evening.
🙋 OR (disagree): I wasn’t. I was playing basketball.
8A I won’t have time to go training tonight.
🙋 OR (disagree): I will. I’m going at six o’clock.
