Activity 4 — Dictionary Skills
The word "way" has different meanings. Match each underlined word to the correct meaning.
way /weɪ/ [C]
1 how you do something — طريقة
Example: There are so many delicious ways to prepare chicken.
2 the road or path to a place, and also part of the name of a road — طريق
Example: The best way to the river is straight ahead, along River Way.
3 the distance from one place to another — مسافة
Example: The shops are only a short way from home.
