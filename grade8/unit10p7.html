الأسرة اللغوية – Nouns of Things & Persons & Activities

الفكرة الأساسية

ما هي الأسرة اللغوية؟

الأسرة اللغوية (Word Family) هي مجموعة من الكلمات تنحدر من نفس الجذر، ونُكوّنها بإضافة لاحقة (suffix) تغيّر وظيفتها في الجملة.
Noun · Thing

اسم الشيء أو المجال

يدل على شيء مجرد أو مجال معرفي أو موضوع.

science · tour · art · guitar
travel · farm · camp · work
Noun · Person

اسم الشخص (الفاعل)

يدل على الشخص الذي يمارس هذا المجال أو النشاط.

scientist · tourist · artist · guitarist
traveller · farmer · camper · worker

اللواحق المستخدمة

Suffixes

-ist
مجالات العلم والفن والموسيقى
science → scientist
art → artist
guitar → guitarist
-er / -r
الأفعال والأنشطة اليومية
farm → farmer
camp → camper
work → worker
drive → driver
-ing
اسم النشاط (activity noun)
farm → farming
dance → dancing
swim → swimming
train → training
⚡ ملاحظة:  -ller تُستخدم مع الأفعال المنتهية بـ ‘l’ مفردة → travel → traveller  |  -er بعد حروف مضعّفة → swim → swimmer

Period 7 – Activity 2

أكمل الجمل – Fill in the Blanks

SENTENCE 1
A It's going to be a huge building, but there's a lot of work still to do.
B Yes, so we have 500 workers here, and they're working day and night!
SENTENCE 2
A In the old days, it was dangerous to travel in the centre of Africa.
B Yes, and travellers were often away from home for months or even years.
SENTENCE 3
A How many tourists come to see Jericho every year?
B Oh, thousands! Lots of them use tourist buses that visit several places.
SENTENCE 4
A Fareeda is very good at maths and science at school.
B Yes, she wants to train to be a scientist when she leaves school.
SENTENCE 5
A Is it true that Fuad is a farmer?
B Yes, he has a farm near Jericho, and he produces fruit and vegetables.
SENTENCE 6
A I've heard that the guitar is your favourite musical instrument.
B Yes, I want to be a guitarist more than anything else in the world!

Period 7 – Activity 4

أكمل الجمل – Fill in the Blanks

SENTENCE 1
A Let's go swimming in the lake.
B Well, only if the water isn't very deep because I'm not a very good swimmer.
SENTENCE 2
A Do we really have to go and do more football training this evening?
B Yes, the trainer says we really do – or we won't win our big match.
SENTENCE 3
A Look at all those different crops. Farming seems very important here.
B It is, and lots of the people in this area are farmers.
SENTENCE 4
A I want to train to be a teacher when I leave school.
B Good! Teaching means helping people to learn, and that's important work.
SENTENCE 5
A In Arab culture, singing is very important.
B Yes, and great singers like Oum Kalthoum can become very famous.
SENTENCE 6
A I like camping up here in the hills. It's good to sleep under the stars!
B Well, lots of other people feel the same. There are campers everywhere!
