المكان 2: بعد (am, is, are)

مكانها بعد الفعل To Be
(am · is · are · aren't · isn't)

⭐ تذكّر: قبل الفعل العادي... وبعد فعل الكينونة (verb to be)!

STEP 1🧠 Activity 1: Read and think, then complete the rules

اقرأ الجمل التالية وانتبه لمكان الكلمات الملونة، ثم اختر الكلمة الصحيحة لإكمال القاعدتين في الأسفل.

1We use always, usually, often, sometimes and never to tell us / something happens.
2We put always, usually, often, sometimes and never / am, are, is, 'm not, aren't, isn't — but you put them / other verbs.

📌 Grammar: adverbs of frequency
• We use always, usually, often, sometimes and never to tell us how often.
• We put them before most verbs but after am, are, is, 'm not, aren't, isn't.
