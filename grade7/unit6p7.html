✍️ Write correct sentencesاكتبِ الجملَ بشكلٍ صحيح

✍️ Writing skills: contracted forms

Remember that we use contractions with pronouns when we speak or when we write something which someone is speaking: They've already scored three goals. We don't use contractions with nouns: The Brazilian team have scored.

نختصرُ مع الضمائر: they've · he's · I've، ولا نختصرُ مع الأسماء: The Brazilian team have scored.
