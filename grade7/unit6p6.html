🎯 Lesson Objectivesأهداف الدرس

👀

Talk about sports

Look at pictures of sports and say which ones you have seen and tried. الحديثُ عن رياضاتٍ شاهدناها وجرّبناها.

🎧

Listen to commentators

Listen to sports commentators and number the pictures. الاستماعُ إلى المعلّقينَ الرياضيّينَ وترقيمُ الصور.

💬

Ask and answer

Ask and answer with the present perfect: Have you scored any goals? السؤالُ والإجابةُ بالمضارعِ التامّ.

💛

Value

Try new sports and cheer for every player. نجرّبُ رياضاتٍ جديدةً ونشجّعُ كلَّ اللاعبين.

2

3
"Have your team won any matches?" — "Yes, they ______."
4
"What sports have you ______ to do?"
5
"Oh, no! I think he ______ himself."
