★ اسحب وأفلت — Match the meaning

الكلمات:

اختر الإجابة الصحيحة لكل سؤال، واقرأ الشرح بالعربي.

4 Listen and read. Underline the words. 5 mins 🎧

استمع واقرأ، وضع خطًا تحت كلمات النشاط الأول التي تسمعها داخل المحادثة.

  1. Say Listen and read. Underline the words from activity 1. Play the recording again as students follow in the book.
  2. Students put up their hands when they hear one of the words from activity 1. Pause for them to underline the words. Rewind when necessary.
  3. Repeat audio activity 3.
الكلمات في المحادثة: ages · hug · kiss · often · perhaps · pleased · smile · surprised

5 Work in groups. Act out the conversations. 8 mins

اعملوا في مجموعات ومثّلوا المحادثتين الواردتين في النشاط الرابع.

  1. Divide the class into groups of four to act out the two conversations. Tell them to decide who will read each of the parts in the conversation. Tell them to read through their part silently.
  2. Say Act out the conversations. Monitor their reading as they perform.
للتعلّم الذاتي: اقرأ كل دور بصوت عالٍ وسجّل نفسك لتتدرّب على النطق.

★ REVIEW 5 mins

المراجعة الختامية.

  1. Choose one group to perform the conversation in front of the class.
  2. Get the students to identify the Everyday English phrases in the conversations and practise them.
