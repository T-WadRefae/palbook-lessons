Lesson Objectives

أهداف الدرس

  • 1
    To identify and pronounce new vocabulary about nature and picnics.
    أن يتعرف الطالب على المفردات الجديدة المتعلقة بالطبيعة والنزهات وينطقها بشكل صحيح.
  • 2
    To listen to a description and identify the correct picnic place.
    أن يستمع الطالب إلى وصف ويحدد مكان النزهة الصحيح.
  • 3
    To read a descriptive passage and understand its main ideas.
    أن يقرأ الطالب فقرة وصفية ويفهم أفكارها الرئيسية.
  • 4
    To recognize what pronouns refer to within a text.
    أن يحدد الطالب مرجع الضمائر داخل النص.
  • 5
    To answer comprehension questions and correct false statements.
    أن يجيب الطالب على أسئلة الفهم ويصحح الجمل الخاطئة.
  • 6
    To talk about a favourite picnic place using the new vocabulary.
    أن يتحدث الطالب عن مكان نزهته المفضل مستخدماً المفردات الجديدة.

Read the Passage

اقرأ الفقرة · مرر فوق الكلمات الحمراء لرؤية معناها · والضمائر الزرقاء لمعرفة ما تعود عليه

كلمة عربية · Arabic meaning
ضمير · Pronoun reference

People often ask me what I like to do at weekends. My answer is always the same: I like to go for a picnic with my family.

Question 1

What does Majed like to do at weekends?

'Oh, do you have a favourite place?' they ask. So I tell them about a beautiful quiet place we know. I tell them about the route that takes you between two mountains.

Question 2

Where does the route take you?

You leave the car and walk along a small path. The narrow path takes you along a valley where there are always lots of flowers. At the end there are some woods with huge old trees. They protect you from the wind and the sun.

Question 3

What is at the end of the path?

There you eat your picnic. You listen to the birds singing and smell the flowers. You enjoy nature with your family. It brings you great happiness.

Question 4

What do you do at the picnic place?

'Oh,' they say. 'It sounds wonderful. Where is it?' But I don't tell them. I don't want it to be popular. It is my place. So no, I won't tell you where it is.

Question 5

Why doesn't Majed tell people where his picnic place is?

Exercise 4 · True or False

Read and tick (✓) the true sentences. Then correct the false ones.

1. Majed likes to go for a picnic with his friends.

Correction: Majed likes to go for a picnic with his family.

2. The picnic place is usually windy.

Correction: The picnic place is usually quiet (the trees protect it from the wind).

3. Majed likes nature.

Great! This sentence is true.

Exercise 5 · Answer the Questions

Read the passage and write your answer. Then check the model answer.

1. Where does the path from the car park take you?

It takes you along a valley.

2. Why doesn't Majed tell people about the picnic place?

Because he doesn't want it to be popular.
