📘 Grammar: present perfect

We form the present perfect with has / have and the past participle. When we form the past participle, we add -ed to regular verbs, such as start, enjoy and learn. Some verbs are irregular, such as be, come and do and we need to learn the past participle. We use the present perfect to talk about a past action with an effect on the present.

I / you / we / they have ('ve) + scoredhe / she / it has ('s) + wonhaven't / hasn't + scored … yetHave you finished? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
نكوّنُ المضارعَ التامَّ من has أو have مع التصريفِ الثالثِ للفعل، ونستعملُه لحدثٍ وقعَ في الماضي وله أثرٌ في الحاضر.

❓ Activity 5: Write questions and answers. Then listen and check.
اضغطِ الكلماتِ بالترتيبِ الصحيحِ لتبنيَ السؤال، ثمَّ الإجابة. اضغطْ كلمةً في السطرِ لتُعيدَها. السؤالُ يبدأُ بـ Have / Has أو What.
1have scored / our team / any goals?
↳Yes / have. / they / have / scored / they / two
🔊 Have our team scored any goals? — Yes, they have. They have scored two.
2the match / has / finished?
↳No / hasn't / it / finished.
🔊 Has the match finished? — No, it hasn't finished.
3You / have / done / your homework?
↳Yes / have. / I
🔊 Have you done your homework? — Yes, I have.
4have you / done / what / today?
↳have / I / had / an amazing breakfast.
🔊 What have you done today? — I have had an amazing breakfast.
5what / visited / have you / in Palestine?
↳I / seen / have / many places.
🔊 What have you visited in Palestine? — I have seen many places.
