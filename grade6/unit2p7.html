🔗 Join with butاربط الجمل بـ but

2) Think and write the correct sentences. Join them using but. اربطِ الجملتين بكلمة but، وابدأْ بحرفٍ كبيرٍ وانتهِ بنقطة.
✏️ Remember: use a CAPITAL letter at the start of a sentence. Use a full stop (.) at the end. Join the two sentences using but. تذكّر: حرفٌ كبيرٌ في البداية، ونقطةٌ في النهاية، ونربط بـ but.
rania lent her favourite book to fatima + but + fatima left the book at home
Rania lent her favourite book to Fatima, but Fatima left the book at home.
rania asked for her book + but + fatima did not have it
Rania asked for her book, but Fatima did not have it.
rania needed her book + but + fatima did not understand
Rania needed her book, but Fatima did not understand.

✍️ Write a Quizاكتب أسئلة كويز

3) Think and write questions and answers you can use in a quiz about friends. اكتبْ أسئلةً وأجوبةً عن الأصدقاءِ يمكنُ استخدامُها في كويز. اكتبْ في المربّعات، ثم انسخْها في دفترك.

⭐ Example

Q: What does a good friend do?

A: A good friend shares and understands you.

Q: What is a problem with friends?

A: When friends argue and do not give back things.
