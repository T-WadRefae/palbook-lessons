📅 Read & Write the Datesاكتب التواريخ

1) Read and write the correct dates. اضغطْ على التاريخِ من البنك، ثم اضغطْ على الفراغِ المناسب.
1 Omar's family were staying on the farm from the to the of July.
2 Rania's family were camping from the fifteenth to the of August.
3 Amy was playing in the river on the of August when her boat turned over.
4 Omar was riding his bike in Jericho on the of August when he fell off.

🔗 Join with whenاربط الجمل بـ when

2) Think and write the correct sentences. Join them using when. اربطِ الجملتين بكلمة when، وابدأْ بحرفٍ كبيرٍ وانتهِ بنقطة.
✏️ Remember: use a CAPITAL letter at the start and a full stop (.) at the end. Join the sentences using when. تذكّر: حرفٌ كبيرٌ في البداية، ونقطةٌ في النهاية، ونربط بـ when.
amy was playing in a boat + when + it turned over
Amy was playing in a boat when it turned over.
omar was riding his bike + when + he fell off
Omar was riding his bike when he fell off.
the family were driving to the farm + when + the wheel burst
The family were driving to the farm when the wheel burst.

✍️ Write About an Adventureاكتب عن مغامرة

3) Think and write sentences about an adventure you had. اكتبْ جملاً عن مغامرةٍ حدثتْ لك، مستخدماً الماضي المستمرَّ و when. اكتبْ في المربّعات ثم انسخْها في دفترك.

⭐ Example

Last summer I had a great adventure. I was hiking in the valley when I saw some baby birds. They were trying to fly. It was wonderful!
