📖 Read & Writeاقرأ واكتب الإجابة

1) Read and write full answers about the pictures. اقرأِ الصورَ واكتبْ إجابةً كاملةً، ثم افتحِ الإجابةَ النموذجيةَ للتأكد.
🐬"I pushed the diver while the other divers were watching."
🐟"I wasn't safe. They were attacking the plants together!"
🐭"Stop that noise!"
1 What did the dolphin do?
ماذا فعل الدولفين؟
💡 Show model answer
The dolphin pushed the diver while the other divers were watching.
2 Why was the orange fish hiding?
لماذا كانت السمكة البرتقالية مختبئة؟
💡 Show model answer
The orange fish hid while the green fish were fighting.
3 Why was the mouse angry?
لماذا كان الفأر غاضباً؟
💡 Show model answer
The mouse was angry because the oxen were fighting / making a noise.

🔗 Join with "While"اربط بـ while

2) Think and write the correct sentences. Join the two parts using while. فكّرْ واكتبِ الجملةَ الصحيحةَ بربطِ الجزأينِ بكلمةِ while.
💡 Remember: use a CAPITAL letter at the start of a sentence. Use a full stop at the end. Join the sentences using while. تذكّرْ: حرفٌ كبيرٌ في البداية، ونقطةٌ في النهاية، والربطُ بكلمةِ while.
1 Join the two parts:
amy's parents watched a film  +  she was doing her homework
💡 Show model answer
Amy's parents watched a film while she was doing her homework.
2 Join the two parts:
the orange fish took the food  +  the green fish were fighting
💡 Show model answer
The orange fish took the food while the green fish were fighting.
3 Join the two parts:
the wheel burst  +  the family were driving to the farm
💡 Show model answer
The wheel burst while the family were driving to the farm.

✍️ Write & Practiseاكتب وتدرّب

3) Think and write sentences about a film you saw. فكّرْ واكتبْ جُملاً عن فيلمٍ شاهدته. استعنْ بالإطاراتِ أدناه.

🎬 Sentence frames:

  • Last week I watched a film about … .
  • It was funny / exciting / sad .
  • While the … was … , the … … .
4) Write the words. Practise your handwriting. Tap 🔊 to hear each word. اكتبِ الكلماتِ وتدرّبْ على خطِّك. اضغطْ 🔊 لسماعِ كلِّ كلمة.
