Grammar

Use the Past Continuous: was / were + verb + -ing. أن يستخدمَ الطالبُ الماضيَ المستمرَّ: was/were + الفعل + ing.

✍️

Writing

Answer the question "What were they doing?" in full sentences. أن يجيبَ عن سؤال «What were they doing?» بجملٍ كاملة.

🗣️

Speaking

Ask and answer questions about a film you saw. أن يسألَ ويجيبَ عن فيلمٍ شاهده مع زميله.

✍️ What were they doing?اكتب الإجابة الصحيحة

2) Think and write the correct answers. Use "was/were + verb-ing". فكّرْ واكتبِ الإجابةَ الصحيحةَ مستخدماً was/were مع الفعل ‎+ing‎.
✅ Example: cat / chase / mouse → The cat was chasing the mouse.
1 fish / hide / plant
The fish … under the plant.

2 oxen / eat / together
The oxen … together.
💡 Show model answer

3 tiger / attack / ox
