👂 Listen & Answerاستمع وأجب

🧔"We were watching a great film last night!"
👨"Oh? Tell me about it."
1) Listen to the conversation and answer the questions. استمعْ إلى الحوارِ ثم أجبْ، وافتحِ الإجابةَ للتأكد. (المهارة استماعٌ — لا يوجد نصٌّ ظاهر)
1 Who's talking?
مَن يتحدّث؟
💡 Show answer
Ben's dad and Omar's dad.
2 What film was Ben's dad talking about?
عن أي فيلمٍ كان والدُ بِن يتحدّث؟
💡 Show answer
The film about the fish.
3 What film was Omar's dad talking about?
عن أي فيلمٍ كان والدُ عمر يتحدّث؟
💡 Show answer
The film about the tiger and the ox.
4 Who watched the funny film?
مَن شاهدَ الفيلمَ المضحك؟
💡 Show answer
Amy.
5 Why couldn't the young ox run away?
لماذا لم يستطعِ الثورُ الصغيرُ الهرب؟
💡 Show answer
Because the tiger was very fast.

⭕ Read, Listen & Circleاختر الصحيح

2) Read. Then listen and circle the correct words. اقرأْ، ثم استمعْ واختر الكلماتِ الصحيحة.
1 The other divers watched while the dolphin / the tired diver.
2 The tiger / the young ox while the older oxen were eating together.
3 The dog watched while the cat / the mouse.
4 The mouse, dog and cat / a noise while the man was sleeping.
5 The orange fish / the food while the green fish were fighting.
