🧵 Using Pronouns for Smooth Writing
~ T. Wad Refae

📌 Lesson Objectives

في نهاية الدرس، سيكون الطالب قادراً على: ✍️

  • 1Use pronouns instead of repeating the same noun.
  • 2Use subject pronouns: he, she, it, they.
  • 3Use object pronouns: him, her, it, them.
  • 4Make each pronoun point clearly to the right noun.
🧵 Today's Mission

تكرار الاسم يجعل النص "مرقّعًا" وخشنًا! 🪡 الضمائر هي الخيط الذي يخيط الجُمل بسلاسة. مهمتنا نستبدل الأسماء المكرّرة بضمائر تشير إليها بوضوح.

🧵 Rough vs. Stitched

قارن بين النصّين، ثم اضغط على كل جزء 👇.

✂️ Rough — repeated nouns
Sami likes books. Sami reads books every day. Sami's friend gave Sami a new book.
🧵 Stitched — with pronouns
Sami likes books. He reads them every day. His friend gave him a new book.
▶🧵 Why use pronouns?
They replace a noun so you don't repeat it — the text flows.

الضمائر تحلّ محلّ الاسم حتى لا نكرّره، فيصبح النص متماسكًا وسلسًا بدل أن يكون مكرّرًا ومملًّا.

▶👤 Subject pronouns — he, she, it, they
They DO the action: He reads. They play.

ضمائر الفاعل تقوم بالفعل: he (هو) · she (هي) · it (لغير العاقل) · they (هم). مثال: She reads.

▶🎯 Object pronouns — him, her, it, them
They RECEIVE the action: I saw him. Give it to them.

ضمائر المفعول تستقبل الفعل وتأتي بعده: him, her, it, them. مثال: I saw them. Give it to her.

▶🎯 Clear reference — who is "he"?
The reader must know which noun the pronoun points to.

يجب أن يكون واضحًا إلى أي اسم يشير الضمير. لا تستخدم he إذا كان هناك أكثر من شخص وقد يلتبس المعنى.

NounSubjectObject
a boy / manhehim
a girl / womansheher
a thingitit
people / thingstheythem

✏️ Thread the Right Pronoun

اختر الضمير الصحيح ليحلّ محلّ الاسم المكرّر 👇

Lina is kind. … helps everyone.
I have two cats. I love … .
Omar lost his pen. Did you see … ?
Sara and I went out. … had fun.
This is Ali's bag. Give it to … .

✅ My Cohesion Check

اضغط على كل بند بعد ما تتأكد منه في كتابتك ✍️

  • ✓I replaced repeated nouns with pronouns.
  • ✓I used he/she/it/they as subjects.
  • ✓I used him/her/it/them as objects.
  • ✓Each pronoun clearly points to the right noun.
  • ✓My text flows smoothly (no clunky repetition).
  • ✓Capital letters and end marks are correct.
