⚙️ Active → Passive

قارن، اقرأ خطوات العملية، ثم اضغط على كل جزء 👇.

⚡ Active
Workers make the chocolate. Then they pack it.
🏭 Passive
The chocolate is made. Then it is packed.
🔧 The Process (passive)
First, the beans are collected. Next, they are cleaned and dried. Then they are ground into powder. Finally, the chocolate is packed and sent to shops.
▶🔧 Form — be + past participle
is/are + V3: is made, are packed, is sent.

المبني للمجهول = be (is/are) + التصريف الثالث للفعل (past participle): is made, are cleaned.

▶🔄 Active → Passive
The object becomes the subject: They make cars → Cars are made.

المفعول به يصبح فاعلًا: They make cars → Cars are made. نضع الشيء في البداية لأنه محور الاهتمام.

▶🏭 Why passive for processes?
We care about the action/thing, not who does it.

في وصف العمليات يهمّنا ما يحدث وليس من يفعله — لذلك نستخدم المجهول ونحذف الفاعل غالبًا.

▶👤 by + agent (optional)
Add "by …" only if the doer is important: …made by machines.

نضيف by + الفاعل فقط إذا كان مهمًّا: The bottles are filled by machines. وإلا نحذفه.

▶🕰️ Passive across the four tenses
Only the form of "be" changes — the past participle (V3) stays the same.

القاعدة الذهبية: نُغيّر صيغة be حسب الزمن فقط، ويبقى التصريف الثالث (V3) ثابتًا. الجدول تحت يوضّح الأزمنة الأربعة.

🕰️ Transformation Across Tenses

نفس الفعل (make → made) في الأزمنة الأربعة: لاحظي كيف تتغيّر be فقط 👇

Tense⚡ Active🏭 Passive = be + V3
Present SimpleThey make it.It is made. / They are made.
Past SimpleThey made it.It was made. / They were made.
Present ContinuousThey are making it.It is being made. / They are being made.
Past ContinuousThey were making it.It was being made. / They were being made.

💡 الخلاصة: Present → is/are (being) + V3  •  Past → was/were (being) + V3. الكلمة being تُضاف في الأزمنة المستمرة فقط.

✏️ Run the Line

اختر الصيغة المبنية للمجهول الصحيحة 👇

Active: "They make cars here." → "Cars … here."
The bottles … by machines.
The bread … in a big oven.
Past Simple — "They built the house in 1990." → "The house … in 1990."
Present Continuous — "A mechanic is repairing the car." → "The car … now."
Past Continuous — "Workers were cleaning the rooms." → "The rooms … all morning."
