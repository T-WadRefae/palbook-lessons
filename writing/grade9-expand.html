✍️ The Expanded Story
One sunny morning, Omar took his little dog to the park. Suddenly, the dog ran away and was lost! Omar searched everywhere and called its name with a worried heart. Finally, he heard a soft bark behind a tree. He was so happy to hold his dog again.
▶🗺️ Plan with notes (who/where/what/end)
Notes are the plan: characters, place, problem, ending.

الملاحظات هي مخطّط القصة: من (الشخصيات)، أين (المكان)، ماذا حدث (الحدث/المشكلة)، والنهاية.

▶🎨 Add details & description
Add adjectives & setting: "a little dog", "one sunny morning".

وسّع كل ملاحظة بالتفاصيل والصفات ووصف المكان والزمان: little dog, sunny morning, big park.

▶💖 Add feelings & a hook
Show emotions and a strong opening/ending: worried, so happy.

أضِف المشاعر (worried, excited, so happy) وافتتاحية جذّابة ونهاية مؤثّرة — هذا ما يجعل القصة حيّة.

▶🔗 Link & sequence the events
Use time/drama words: One morning, Suddenly, Then, Finally.

اربط اللقطات بكلمات الزمن والتشويق: One morning, Suddenly, Then, After that, Finally — لتتدفّق القصة.
