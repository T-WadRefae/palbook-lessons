📌 Lesson Objectives

في نهاية الدرس، رح يكون الطالب قادر على: ✍️

  • 1Join two sentences with but to show contrast.
  • 2Join two sentences with because to give a reason.
  • 3Choose the right connector for the meaning.
  • 4Write longer, connected sentences.
🪙 Today's Mission

عملتنا لها وجهان: وجه but يُظهر التناقض (فكرة مختلفة)، ووجه because يُعطي السبب (ليش). مهمتنا نختار الوجه الصحيح ونربط جُملنا!

🪙 Two Faces, Two Jobs

اقرأ النموذج، ثم اضغط على كل جزء 👇.

I wanted to play, but it was raining. 🔄
We stayed at home because it was raining. 💡
The cake is small, but it is delicious. 🔄
I am happy because today is a holiday. 💡

▶🔄 "but" — shows a CONTRAST
Use "but" when the second idea is different / surprising.

نستخدم but عندما تكون الفكرة الثانية مختلفة أو مفاجئة. مثال: The bag is old, but it is strong. (نضع فاصلة قبل but عادةً).

▶💡 "because" — gives a REASON
Use "because" to answer WHY (the reason).

نستخدم because لنعطي السبب (نجاوب على سؤال: ليش؟). مثال: I am tired because I ran fast.

▶🧩 The structure
Sentence 1 + but / because + Sentence 2.

الشكل: جملة + (but / because) + جملة. كل جزء لازم يكون فيه فاعل وفعل. مثال: She is small + but + she is fast.

▶🤔 How to choose
Different idea → but · The reason → because.

اسأل نفسك: هل الفكرة الثانية مختلفة/عكس المتوقع؟ → استخدم but. هل هي السبب؟ → استخدم because.

✏️ Flip the Right Coin

اختر الرابط الصحيح لكل جملة: but (تناقض) أم because (سبب)؟ 👇

I like apples … I don't like bananas.
She was tired … she worked all day.
The film is good … it is very long.
We use an umbrella … it is raining.
He is young … he is very clever.

✅ My Writing Check

اضغط على كل بند بعد ما تتأكد منه في كتابتك ✍️

  • ✓I used but for a different / opposite idea.
  • ✓I used because to give a reason (why).
  • ✓Each part has a subject and a verb.
  • ✓I put a comma before but when needed.
  • ✓My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • ✓My sentence ends with a full stop (.).
