💼 A Model Business Letter

اقرأ الرسالة، ثم اضغط على كل جزء 👇.

Birzeit School, Palestine
5th May, 2025
Mr Adam Khan
Sales Manager
Global Books Ltd
Re: Order of English Books
Dear Mr Khan,
I am writing to order 30 English readers for our school library. Please send the books and the invoice to the address above. I look forward to your reply.
Yours sincerely,
Lina Saleh
Head of Library
▶🏷️ Subject line — Re: …
A short underlined line telling the topic: Re: Order of English Books.

سطر الموضوع يخبر القارئ بموضوع الرسالة قبل قراءتها. اكتبه قصيراً وضع تحته خطاً: Re: …

▶👔 Formal greeting
Dear Mr/Ms + surname, OR Dear Sir or Madam, (if unknown).

التحية الرسمية: Dear Mr/Ms + اسم العائلة, وإذا لم تعرف الاسم: Dear Sir or Madam,

▶✒️ Closing — sincerely vs faithfully
Know the name → Yours sincerely. Don't → Yours faithfully.

إذا عرفت اسم الشخص تختم بـ Yours sincerely, وإذا لم تعرفه (Dear Sir or Madam) تختم بـ Yours faithfully,

▶🪪 Name + job title
Full name on one line, job title under it: Head of Library.

بعد الخاتمة نكتب الاسم الكامل، وتحته المسمّى الوظيفي (مثل: Head of Library).
