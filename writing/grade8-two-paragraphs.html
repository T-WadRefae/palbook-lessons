📌 Lesson Objectives

  • 1Make one paragraph longer & richer with details and examples.
  • 2Plan a second paragraph about a new point.
  • 3Link the two paragraphs with linking words.
  • 4Keep each paragraph on one main idea.

🌉 Two Paragraphs, One Bridge

اقرأ الفقرتين وكلمة الربط بينهما، ثم اضغط على كل جزء 👇.

Paragraph 1 — first point
My school is a great place to learn. The classrooms are big and bright. For example, each room has a smart board and many books. The teachers are kind and always ready to help.
⬇  🔗 In addition,  ⬇
Paragraph 2 — new point
In addition, our school has wonderful activities. We have a football team and an art club. These activities make every day fun and exciting.
▶🧱 Make paragraph 1 richer
Add details, examples and reasons to the same idea: "For example…"

وسّع الفقرة الأولى بإضافة تفاصيل وأمثلة وأسباب عن نفس الفكرة: For example… / This is because…

▶🌉 Start paragraph 2 with a NEW topic sentence
The 2nd paragraph has its own new main idea.

الفقرة الثانية لها فكرة رئيسية جديدة وجملة موضوع خاصة بها (نقطة مختلفة عن الأولى).

▶🔗 Linking words between paragraphs
Add a point: In addition, Also, Furthermore. Contrast: However, On the other hand.

للإضافة: In addition, Also, Furthermore. للتناقض: However, On the other hand. نضعها في بداية الفقرة الثانية.

▶🎯 One idea per paragraph
Don't mix the two points — keep each paragraph focused.

لا تخلط الفكرتين في فقرة واحدة. كل فقرة تبقى على فكرتها لتكون الكتابة منظّمة وواضحة.
