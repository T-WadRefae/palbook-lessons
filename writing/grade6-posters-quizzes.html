📌 Lesson Objectives

في نهاية الدرس، رح يكون الطالب قادر على: ✍️

  • 1Design a clear instructions poster (park rules).
  • 2Write rules using imperatives and Don't.
  • 3Use Please and must / mustn't for polite or strong rules.
  • 4Write quiz questions with question words and a "?".

🪧 A Model Poster + Quiz

اقرأ الملصق والمسابقة، ثم اضغط على كل جزء 👇.

🌳 PARK RULES 🌳
  • ✅ Keep the park clean.
  • 🚫 Don't pick the flowers.
  • 🙏 Please use the bins.
  • ⛔ You must keep dogs on a lead.
❓ PARK QUIZ ❓
  • How many trees are in the park?
  • When does the park close?
▶✅ Imperatives — start with a verb
Keep the park clean. · Use the bins. · Plant a tree.

القانون أو الإرشاد يبدأ بـ فعل أمر مباشرة (بدون فاعل): Keep…, Use…, Close….

▶🚫 Negative rules — Don't
Don't + verb → Don't drop litter. · Don't pick the flowers.

للمنع نستخدم Don't + الفعل: Don't drop litter. (لا ترمِ النفايات).

▶🙏 Polite & strong — Please / must
Please use the bins. · You must keep dogs on a lead. · You mustn't make fires.

Please تجعل الإرشاد مهذباً. must للقانون القوي (إجباري)، وmustn't للممنوع تماماً.

▶❓ Quiz questions — question word + ?
What / Where / When / Who / How many … ? Always end with "?".

سؤال المسابقة يبدأ بكلمة استفهام (What, Where, When, How many…) وينتهي دائماً بعلامة ؟ (?).
